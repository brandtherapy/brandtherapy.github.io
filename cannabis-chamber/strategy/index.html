Section A

Strategy

Where cannabis business connects.

Product

Chamber business infrastructure

The Cannabis Chamber of Commerce is a global nonprofit trade association and active business network for the cannabis and hemp industry. It serves as business infrastructure for industry growth by bringing together operators, entrepreneurs, service providers, investors, sponsors, policymakers, and industry leaders through executive networking, strategic partnerships, education, advocacy and policy insight, regional and global industry access, collaboration, and leadership development.

People

Serious leaders building cannabis business

The Chamber serves serious cannabis and hemp leaders: operators, executives, entrepreneurs, investors, policymakers, sponsors, regional leaders, ancillary partners, innovators, professional organizations, and qualified businesses looking to participate in, grow within, collaborate across, and help shape the future of the industry.

Purpose

Accelerate profitable cannabis growth

The Chamber exists to accelerate profitable growth for cannabis and hemp businesses by expanding access to markets, leadership, partnerships, policy insight, regional and global networks, collaboration, and trusted industry relationships.

Promise

Connections

Connections. The Chamber's promise is to turn serious cannabis business relationships into useful access, collaboration, and opportunity. It helps cannabis and hemp professionals show up credibly, connect constructively, learn the market, advocate responsibly, build strategic partnerships, collaborate with purpose, and create business value through trusted relationships.

Personality

Operator-led institution

Professional, confident, credible, and direct. Operator-led, standards-driven, rational, transparent, business-first, and welcoming to serious operators, leaders, partners, and organizations focused on outcomes, collaboration, and long-term industry progress.
