Public Sans

Outfit carries the name and the headlines. Public Sans carries the words a person has to read.

Use

  • Use Cannabis Chamber Of Commerce on every public piece.
  • Keep ivory or white as the field.
  • Use the master mark and the locked wordmarks.
  • Let a signature line leave the edge, pass behind a solid shape, or stop on a real divider.
  • Photograph people in working rooms.

Leave out

  • Dark blue pages.
  • CCC as a public wordmark.
  • Leaf graphics, smoke, and party scenes.
  • A redrawn small mark.
  • Decorative tribal motifs.
