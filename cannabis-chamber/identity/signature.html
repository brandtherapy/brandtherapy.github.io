Common Ground signature rules

Continuous routes and visible boundaries

The signature lines represent continuous routes. A route must never appear to start or stop in the middle of an uninterrupted background.

Every route entering or leaving an application must meet one of these conditions:

  • It continues beyond the visible viewport or artboard edge and is cropped there.
  • It passes behind an opaque surface with a visibly different color or image, so its disappearance reads as occlusion.
  • It reaches a clearly contrasting divider that defines the edge of the signature field.

A canvas edge, section boundary or mask on the same background color does not count as a visible edge. Never expose flat line ends against that continuous surface. This applies at the top, bottom and sides, including where navigation meets a hero, where a sticky field enters or exits, and where adjacent sections share the same background.

Extend route geometry past the field bounds. Clip the whole field inside its owning section and keep following surfaces opaque. A sticky or fixed layer must never spill over neighboring copy. Preserve uniform scaling and the approved circular nodes and their separation rings.

Animated impulses may taper along the continuous guide routes, as in the selected motion treatment. Their motion envelope does not authorize cutting off the underlying route. The approved node separation rings remain intentional parts of the device.

Required application check

Read back every outer edge at desktop and mobile sizes. For motion, inspect entry, the middle, exit and reverse scrolling. Check both header-to-signature and signature-to-content transitions. Reject any composition where a route looks severed within a continuous background, even when the underlying canvas is technically clipped.

This rule applies to static exports, animated compositions, and website integrations.
