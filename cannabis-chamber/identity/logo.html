Section B · Logo

Logo

The Cannabis Chamber Of Commerce mark and wordmark. The mark is three open C forms in forest, teal and coral convening around a single brass dot, common ground stated as geometry. The wordmark spells the full name in Outfit 600. The Common Ground Field signature is a separate device on its own page: the logo names the brand, the signature is felt without naming it.

03
Construction rules

Geometry, spacing and source files

These are the non-negotiable measurements behind the logo system.

Source geometry

The mark rebuilds from logo-construction/geometry.json and logo-construction/geometry-production.json through logo-construction/build_production.py. Do not hand-edit exported SVGs.

Mark construction

1000-unit canvas, center at 500/500. One master C module is built once, then repeated at 270, 30 and 150 degrees. Outer radius 250, inner radius 154.51, orbit 208.33, dot radius 61.

Terminal clearance

Each active C terminal is an arc concentric with the brass dot. The clearance is 27 construction units, so the end face holds a constant whitespace relationship to the node.

Clear-space unit

One dot diameter is the spacing unit: 122 construction units, about 13 percent of real mark height. The same unit drives clear space and lockup gaps.

Wordmark geometry

Outfit 600 is baked to outlines. Line advance is 1.2 cap heights. The V7 and V8 lockups anchor to measured ink bounds, not the parametric box: top 41.02, bottom 854.49, midline 447.76.

Favicon rule

The favicon is the master mark rendered at true 48, 32 and 24 px. The redrawn small variant is retired and must not re-enter the public system without a new approval gate.

Production route

If the geometry, brass calibration, lockup spacing or favicon tier changes, regenerate the whole production set from build_production.py and refresh the Brand OS outputs from source.

04
Clear space

Breathing room

Keep this minimum space clear on every side of the mark.

Keep clear space of one dot diameter, about 13 percent of mark height, on every side of the mark and lockup. Nothing crosses into it.Minimum mark size 32 px on screen so the C counters and dot stay open. The master mark holds at every size; there is no separate small variant.The favicon is the master mark itself, rendered at 48 / 32 / 24 px. No redrawn geometry.Lockup spacing is constructed from the mark and dot dimensions, never typeset by eye. Do not re-space or re-scale the parts.
05
Misuse

What breaks the mark

These are the failures that make the mark unrecognizable or off-brand.

Never

Do not recolor the mark outside its forest, teal, coral and brass system, the single-ink mono, or the all-ivory inverse.Do not add gradients, glows, drop shadows, or outlines to the mark or wordmark.Do not stretch, condense, rotate, or skew the mark or lockup.Do not place the color mark on forest, ink, a busy photo, or a low-contrast field. Use the inverse mark instead.Do not set "CCC" as a public wordmark, or re-typeset the name as a logo substitute. Use the constructed lockups.Do not color the initials in the two-line lockup. The letterforms carry their module colors in the three-line lockup only; everywhere else the wordmark is single-ink or inverse.Do not swap the mark in for the Common Ground Field signature. The logo names the brand; the signature is the connective field.
