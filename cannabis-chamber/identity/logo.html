03
Clear space

One C on every side

The dashed box is the minimum empty margin. The lines sit in that margin and stop before the artwork.

Measure one C, the forest form at the top of the mark. Its width is the minimum empty margin on the top, bottom, left, and right.

That margin applies to the mark alone and to every lockup. Type, photography, rules, and other logos stay outside it.

The PNG margin is only there so the artwork does not sit on the file edge. It is smaller than this rule. Build the full margin in the layout.

04
Size

Smallest useful size

Below this, the mark stops reading as three forms and a dot.

Use the master mark at every size, including the favicon. Do not swap in a redraw.

Do not render the mark below 24 px. From 32 px up, the three forms and the dot stay distinct.

Keep lockup type at a cap height of 10 px or more.

05
Misuse

What breaks the mark

These are the failures that make the mark unrecognizable or off-brand.

Never
