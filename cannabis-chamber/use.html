Use this OS with AI

The Chamber runs on one file

Everything in this Brand OS compiles into one file, ai-context.md. Paste it into ChatGPT or Claude before asking for copy, event material, sponsor language, or member communication. No file, no standard.

The loop

One clean context file first, then one specific ask. That is how the Chamber stays standards-first instead of sounding like generic cannabis networking.

01

Open

Open ChatGPT or Claude. Start a fresh chat. One chat per task. Long chats drift, especially around voice, claims, and event details.

02

Paste

Hit Copy the brain above and send ai-context.md as the first message. It carries the strategy, voice rules, visual guardrails, and approval discipline.

03

Ask

Say what you need in one line: channel, audience, event or offer, and the real facts available. Or start from a prompt below.

Starter prompts

Copy one, replace the brackets, send. Each prompt keeps public claims, membership value, and advocacy language inside the approved source.

Events

Event promotion

Draft event promotion copy for the Cannabis Chamber Of Commerce.

Event: [name]
Date and time: [confirmed details]
Location: [confirmed venue and city]
Audience: [operators, sponsors, policymakers, service providers, members]
One real reason to attend: [specific business value]

Follow the event and voice rules in this context. Use Cannabis Chamber Of Commerce publicly, not CCC. Do not invent sponsors, speakers, attendance numbers, RSVP links, venue details, or outcomes. Draft only for review.
Membership

Member invitation

Write a membership invitation.

Audience: [who this is for]
What they need from the Chamber: [access, education, sponsorship, policy insight, trusted relationships]
Confirmed benefit or next step: [what is actually live]

Follow the membership and voice rules in this context. Make connection and collaboration concrete. Do not promise leads, revenue, investment, legal outcomes, regulatory wins, or guaranteed introductions. Draft only.
Sponsorship

Sponsor outreach

Draft a sponsor outreach note.

Sponsor: [organization or person]
Event or program: [confirmed name]
Why they fit: [specific business reason]
What is confirmed: [assets, audience, date, package, or next call]

Keep it concise and operator-led. Name the business value without overclaiming reach or results. Do not send this. Stage it for human review.
Advocacy insight

Advocacy insight brief

Draft a short advocacy insight brief.

Issue: [policy, regulation, market access, or business standard]
What is at stake: [specific impact on operators or trusted business activity]
Evidence available: [facts, source, or observed pattern]
Audience: [members, policymakers, sponsors, public]

Use advocacy insight language, not unsupported lobbying claims. Cite facts that are provided and flag missing evidence instead of inventing it. Draft only.

The standard still applies

  • The AI drafts. The Chamber team and Florian review. Nothing sends, posts, publishes, or becomes client-facing straight from a chat.
  • Use Cannabis Chamber Of Commerce in public-facing copy. CCC is internal shorthand only.
  • Do not invent names, dates, venues, sponsors, member benefits, attendance, metrics, testimonials, RSVP links, or program mechanics.
  • Do not promise leads, revenue, investment, legal outcomes, regulatory victories, introductions, sponsor results, or capital outcomes.
  • Use advocacy insight unless a specific approved source supports stronger language.
  • When the source changes, recopy the file. Old paste, old Chamber.
