Signature, in use.

The signature is a connective field, not a placed logo — sweeping arcs that meet at brass nodes, the “common ground” where many paths converge. It comes in two inks from one system: color for light surfaces, and a monochrome version for dark. On dark it lives tone-on-tone and very subtle, the same green a shade lighter, like a watermark pressed into the surface — never a loud graphic.

Rule 1 · It always bleeds

The field runs off every edge of the frame. Lines never dead-end inside the artwork; they exit the frame. Always over-scale and bleed, keep a clear margin for the lockup and copy.

Rule 2 · Quiet on dark

On dark surfaces use the monochrome field tone-on-tone at ~8–12%. Same hue as the background, just lifted. No two-color nodes, no bright ivory lines, nothing that competes.
