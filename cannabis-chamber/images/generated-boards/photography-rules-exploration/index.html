Photography Registers: one sample of each

Four candidate registers for the new Cannabis Chamber photography rules. All four follow the locked boundaries: bright light, cream/green/coral palette, no dark blue, no dark default, no leaf-as-hero, no consumption cues. Casting rule: business hipsters and hippies, professional and deal-focused but every person visibly unique. Pick which lead the rules, which support, and which die.

REGISTER 1

Daylight Documentary

Candid operators mid-deal. Natural window light, airy rooms, photojournalistic 35mm. The workhorse register for web, decks, and event recaps.

REGISTER 2

Membership Pride Portrait

Members on cream seamless, direct gaze, crisp even light. A consistent "faces of the industry" system for spotlights, board pages, and social.

REGISTER 3

Connection Close-up

The connection is the subject: hands, handshakes, shared documents. Sunlit, shallow depth. Emotional register for campaigns and section breaks.

REGISTER 4

Bright Scale + People

Facilities and floors shot wide and high-key, always with people active in frame. Shows business lift and legitimacy without industrial gravity.

Casting note (Florian, 2026-06-12): "business hipsters and hippies." Everyone is business-oriented and self-aware, but visibly unique: tattoos, braids, dreads, leopard print, vintage, turquoise, bolo ties, statement glasses. Casual is the default register; suits are the exception. Hold the line at consumption cues, smoke, tie-dye, and psychedelic visuals: those stay forbidden. Generated 2026-06-12 with Nano Banana Pro as register samples, not final assets.
