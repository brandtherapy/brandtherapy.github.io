The decision on this page: approve the constructed geometry of the D2 mark. One master C module, duplicated by computed rotation at exact 120 degree spacing around a centered brass dot. Approval here locks the geometry and unlocks gate 2: production files and the wordmark lockup. This page does not ask you to approve final color calibration, the small-size variant, or any application use.

05

Scale behavior

Actual raster renders at 200, 64, 32 and 16 pixels.

Known limit, named honestly: the mark holds at 32px and above. At 16px the dot and its clearances merge. The approved composition has a 27 unit counter, below the 125 unit favicon minimum. Recommendation for gate 2: a dedicated small-size variant (larger dot, wider clearances) for 24px and below. The master geometry on this page stays faithful to the approved mark.
