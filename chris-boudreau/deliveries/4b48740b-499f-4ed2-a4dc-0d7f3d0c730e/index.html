Your Strategy

Your positioning

Positioning statement

Market foresight. Steadfast leadership.

Chris connects economic and regulatory change with where investment and market demand are heading. He uses that perspective to set direction and build stronger businesses, with a personal commitment to the people and work he takes on.

Category
Executive leadership
Distinctive idea
Market foresight. Steadfast leadership.
Founder role
Chris brings his own judgment and commitment to the businesses he leads.

The Focus Star

What your brand stands for

  1. Product Business leadership that connects economics, regulation, and investment to anticipate market shifts and set long-term direction.

    Chris connects changes in the economy, regulation and investment with the opportunities facing a business. His value is the breadth of that judgment and the direction it gives a company.

  2. People Boards, business owners, and progressive operators seeking a leader who sees beyond the company and understands where the market is going.

    The audience has responsibility for a company’s future and needs leadership that understands the market around it. The opportunity may be a leadership appointment, expansion or a changing competitive landscape.

  3. Purpose Build stronger businesses, teams, and relationships that can anticipate and shape the future of the market.

    Chris builds businesses, teams and relationships that can anticipate market change and help determine what comes next.

  4. Promise Lead with unwavering commitment, take ownership, and follow through on every undertaking.

    You stay engaged, accept responsibility and follow through. The people building the business can count on your continued commitment as circumstances change.

  5. Personality Far-sighted, analytical, steadfast, enterprising, and caring.

    He considers the wider implications of a decision, looks for opportunity and takes his responsibilities seriously. Care and personal dependability give his executive judgment human credibility.

Point of view

A company’s future depends on understanding the market around it and taking responsibility for what comes next.

Brand archetype

Steady and responsible

You take responsibility and remain involved. Your analytical judgment is balanced by care for the people building the business.

  1. Hero leads. Take responsibility for the work you agree to do.
  2. Caregiver and Sage support. Listen carefully and explain the reasoning behind your advice.
  3. Friend adds warmth. Be approachable and easy to talk to.

Keep the tone practical. Do not claim that you can predict the market, and do not promise a result.

Voice principles

Executive perspective, clear language

01

Explain the implications

Connect an external development to a decision facing the business.

How would a change in financing costs affect our expansion options?
02

Make your judgment clear

Give a considered view and the reasoning behind it.

I would assess the regulatory outlook before committing capital.
03

Speak with personal accountability

Say what you will take responsibility for.

I will stay involved and follow through on the commitments we make.
04

Keep people in the picture

Consider what a decision means for the team as well as the business.

What will the team need to make this direction work?

Starter prompts

Prompts for common tasks

01

Write a LinkedIn post

Using the brand context above, write a LinkedIn post about one supplied economic or regulatory development and its implications for business direction. Under 150 words. Distinguish facts from your interpretation and do not invent forecasts.

02

Plan a month of posts

Using the brand context above, draft a four-week LinkedIn plan with two to three posts per week. Rotate the three content territories, give each post a one-line angle and a format (text post, short video, or document), and keep every angle inside the factual boundaries.

03

Draft the offer statement

Using the brand context above, write a short explanation of Chris's executive work for a board choosing who leads a company. Use the supplied offer and career facts. Do not add services, prices, or outcomes.

04

Shape a collaboration

Using the brand context above, write a short message proposing a joint perspective piece with an operator or firm active in a market Chris knows. Explain what Chris brings, what the other side brings, and why it is useful to publish together. Plain, direct, no hard sell.

05

Build a campaign brief

Using the brand context above, draft three LinkedIn posts about market foresight, building stronger businesses and accountable leadership. Use supplied facts only and do not invent client stories.

06

Prepare warm outreach

Using the brand context above, write a short message Chris can send to ten people he already knows who fit the audience or know someone who does. Name the opportunity in one sentence, ask one question, and make replying easy. Under 80 words.
