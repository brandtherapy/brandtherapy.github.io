The Focus Star

The brand in five answers

  1. Product Financial security and peace of mind for the family.

    Jenina uses life insurance with living benefits, retirement plans, and living trusts to support a mom's financial security.

  2. People Latina single moms running the household alone.

    Persona: The mom carrying it all alone. Kids and bills on one income, stable enough to care, and nobody has ever sat with her on the money.

  3. Purpose Prepare one-income families for financial disruption.

    Jenina helps families understand their coverage and prepare before an emergency.

  4. Promise Put yourself first.

    Brand DNA: Put yourself first. A mom's own security is what lets her take care of everyone else.

  5. Personality Approachable and caring, with a classic feminine look and restrained presentation

    Jenina is warm in the room and precise on the numbers, with classic restraint. Faith and family may appear naturally in Jenina's voice; the sales case stays practical.

Brand archetype

The caregiver with standards

Eight words define the brand: caring, authoritative, confident, desirable, knowledgeable, loyal, trustworthy, safe. Caregiver leads; Friend and Ruler add approachability and standards.

  1. Caregiver leads. Jenina protects the family by starting with the mom.
  2. Friend and Ruler carry it. Friend makes Jenina easy to call: loyal, relatable, English y Español. Ruler adds standards: order, confidence, a plan that holds.
  3. Sage, Innocent, and Lover support. Sage keeps the numbers precise. Innocent keeps the language honest and reassuring. Lover adds restraint and class to Jenina's presentation.
  4. Hero stays a trace. It adds measured resilience to the voice.

Avoid provocation, comedy, money jokes, and promises that sound like magic.
