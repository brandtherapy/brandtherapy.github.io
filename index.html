The Focus Star.

Five edges, one center. Not a worksheet, a working map of how your brand actually decides things.

  • Product. What people actually pay for, not the mission version.
  • People. Who you do your best work for, and who you don't.
  • Purpose. The real reason this exists, traced to something concrete.
  • Personality. How it sounds when it's not performing.
  • Promise. The one thing that has to be true every time.

Workshop exercise

Where does your brand sit?

Your brand is not your logo. It is everything people meet: how you speak, how you write, what the website looks like, what you wear, how the invoice reads. Drag each dot to where your brand actually lives today. Not where your industry says it should sit.

Personality Attributes.

Pick every word that describes your brand as it is today, at least 80 percent of the time. Not the brand you're building toward. If you hesitate on a word, it doesn't make the cut. Most people land between 10 and 20.
