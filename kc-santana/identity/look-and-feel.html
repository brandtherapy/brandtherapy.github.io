02
Type system

Typography

Cachet type system: Barlow Condensed carries editorial warmth and major brand headlines; Barlow carries navigation, body text, labels, forms, and practical service detail. The renderer must not fall back to FP typography.

Editorial display
Barlow Condensed

Use: brand headlines, expressive lines, source-board phrases, footer promise, and warm high-touch moments.

Editorial echo
IBM Plex Mono

Use: mixed-headline emphasis and large pull moments. Keep it elegant and spacious, not ornate.

Functional sans
Barlow

Use: body text, form fields, small proof lines, and practical advisory copy. Keep it plain, legible, and human.

03
System · Rules

Do & Don't

Non-negotiable. These rules exist to maintain visual coherence across every touchpoint.

Do

Show training, recovery, and the functional stack in real SoCal daylight.

Do

Treat cannabis like equipment: measured, labeled, controlled.

Do

Use command language and proof: schedules, receipts, visible routine.

Do

Keep femininity strong: form, presence, capability.

Do

Weave the special-needs-mom layer through lifestyle content as lived proof.

Do

Keep surfaces warm-bone, sharp-edged, and rationally annotated.

Don't

No weed leaves as a primary mark, no smoke glamour, no 420 cues.

Don't

No beach-influencer softness, no spa wellness, no pastel therapy.

Don't

No pink Barbie, no cutesy empowerment, no sparkle.

Don't

No GLP-1 or weight-loss shortcut imagery, no scales, no before-after bodies.

Don't

No medical clinic language, no white coats, no pill bottles.

Don't

No sobby special-needs-mom lead stories, and never her son's face.

04
Imagery · Photography

Photography

How real photos and AI-generated imagery should look.

Image modes:

  • KC as the standard: strong, present, unsentimental, camera-aware but never influencer-cute. She is the proof, photographed like an athlete, not a host.
  • Performance ritual: outdoor SoCal training: track, hills, trails, weights, sweat, water, grip, hard daylight.
  • Recovery ritual: the controlled stack: recovery table, magnesium, mushrooms, sun-grown cannabis as a measured object, stretching, breath.
  • High-standard room: wellness events, mastermind dinners, speaking and podcast tables: serious women, serious conversation, KC at the head of the room.
  • Evidence surfaces: checklists, schedules, weights, labeled jars, water, hard shadows: the receipts of discipline.

Treatment: hard light, true color, sharp focus, honest sweat. Crops are tight and geometric. Overlays come from the data-strip system, not from filters.

Forbidden imagery: weed leaves as the main mark, smoke clouds, joints as personality, tie-dye, peace signs, munchies; pill bottles, white coats, clinical pharmacy; scales, before-after bodies, miracle weight-loss cues; hearts, sparkles, princess pastels, glitter; soft pastel therapy wellness; her son's face.

05
Categories the brand refuses

Anti-references

What this brand is visually and categorically NOT, framed as anti-targets for AI image generation, layout work, and brand positioning.

Stoner lifestyle brand

Couch, smoke, munchies, basement light. KC's cannabis is sun-grown, measured, and athletic. The aesthetic of laziness is the opposite of the promise.

Beach influencer

Golden-hour softness, bikini-lifestyle ease, effortless living. KC's SoCal is terrain and training, not vibes.

Spa wellness retreat

Calm, candles, eucalyptus, whispered self-care. This brand demands effort; it does not soothe people out of it.

Pharma-glossy wellness

Clinical white, pill-bottle promises, medicalized shortcuts. KC sells behavior, not prescriptions.

Pink empowerment brand

Barbie pink, glitter, yass-queen energy. Femininity here is strength and command, not cuteness.

GLP-1 era marketing

Shortcut bodies, before-after panels, effortless transformation. The entire brand exists to refuse this.
