Direction Two.

Sun-Grown Athlete

A SoCal performance direction where cannabis becomes part of a disciplined outdoor fitness and recovery ritual.

Statusproposal SourceBuilt from approved Focus Star and slider data. No logo exploration. Proposal material only; do not apply this palette or type energy to the Brand OS shell. CenterNo Excuses

02
Strategy translation

What this direction is saying.

This direction makes the cannabis bridge useful without letting cannabis take over. It reframes sun-grown weed, functional mushrooms, recovery, training, and women's longevity as one disciplined performance world.

Best Fit

Cannabis positioning, outdoor training content, recovery ritual posts, future SKU exploration, and lifestyle photography without stoner cues.

Watchout

If too sunny and soft, it can drift into wellness-guru calm. It needs athletic tension, rational overlays, and hard discipline language.
