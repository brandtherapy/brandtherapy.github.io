Voice

How the brand speaks. Principles first, then what's banned, then exemplars.

01
Operating rules

Voice principles

Voice is part of identity. The strategic decision about Personality lives in STRATEGY.md; the operational voice rules live here.

Principles

  • Rough-edged. Plainspoken. Unsentimental.
  • Tough-love and demanding, not nurturing.
  • Serious before playful. Rational before emotional. Transparent before mysterious. Literal before abstract.
  • Commanding without body shame. The target is behavior, never bodies.
  • Feminine through strength and presence, not cuteness.
  • Cannabis as discipline-stack tool, not a flag.

No-excuse language system

KC is the product. Authority comes from thought leadership and example-setting, never from coaching packages or curriculum language. "Run the standard" means show up and do it: the copy shows KC doing the work and lets the example issue the command.

  • Lead with proof: the run happened, the stack is measured, the schedule is visible.
  • Short sentences. Direct commands. Hard contrast.
  • Refuse shortcuts by name when needed (GLP-1s, hacks, passive wellness), without moralizing at individual people.
  • Never soften the standard into generic empowerment or motivational poster language.

Special-needs-mom intertwine rule

From the 2026-06-10 session: the special-needs-mom layer is woven organically through fitness and healthy-lifestyle content. It is lived context that raises the standard, never the story being sold.

  • Correct: training framed as "free therapy for a special needs mom." Correct: bringing the kids to the marathon instead of skipping it.
  • It appears mid-content, as one thread of proof among many. It NEVER leads a piece, never becomes a sobby opening, and never asks for sympathy.
  • Her son's face is never used. The brand never becomes mommy-first.

On-voice examples

  • "No shortcuts. Do the work."
  • "The routine is not cute. That is why it works."
  • "Cannabis belongs in the discipline stack, not on a basement couch."
  • "If the body keeps receipts, stop acting surprised by the bill."
  • "Show up. No exceptions."
  • "Saturday long run. Free therapy for a special needs mom."
  • "Race day. The kids came with me."

Off-voice examples

  • "Yass queen, time to glow."
  • "Your wellness journey starts with self-love."
  • "Cannabis vibes for your soft era."
  • "Unlock your best self with effortless transformation."
  • "As a special needs mom, some days I just want to cry. Here's my story."

Banned characters

  • em-dash
  • en-dash

Plain hyphen-minus is fine.

Banned phrases and tropes

  • Yass queen.
  • Soft era.
  • Weed girl.
  • Mommy wellness.
  • Namaste.
  • Shortcut body.
  • Miracle weight loss.
  • Stoner chic.
  • Cannabis goddess.
