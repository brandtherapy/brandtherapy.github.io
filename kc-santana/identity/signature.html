The Performance Data Strip

The signature is a thin performance data strip on the bottom edge of an asset. It reads like equipment labeling: a Trail Black label, one accent tick, and mono metrics on a Water Steel band. One strip per asset. Never decorative.

Geometry

  • Strip height h = max(56px, 5.2% of image height).
  • Full bleed: the strip runs edge to edge across the bottom of the asset.
  • Sharp corners only. No radius, no shadow, no transparency.
  • The strip sits on the final crop. It may overlap the photographic crop line; the photo never overlaps the strip.

Label segment

  • Trail Black #1B1712 rectangle anchored to the left edge.
  • Text: Barlow SemiBold, all caps, Sun Bone #F1E2C7, size 0.40 x h, vertically centered.
  • Horizontal padding 0.55 x h on both sides of the label text.
  • Content: who or what owns the asset. KC SANTANA, RUN THE STANDARD, HILL DAY, TODAY.

Accent tick

  • Solid vertical tick immediately after the label segment: 0.14 x h wide, full strip height.
  • Performance Green #4E6B46 is the default.
  • Heat Clay #B55336 only when the content is effort or intensity: hill days, no-excuses statements, event pushes.
  • Exactly one tick per strip. The tick is the only color moment in the strip.

Metrics field

  • Background: Water Steel #405C73 for the remainder of the strip.
  • Text: IBM Plex Mono Medium, all caps, Sun Bone, size 0.34 x h, starting 0.55 x h after the tick.
  • Separator between values: a middle dot with spaces, · . No commas, no slashes, no dashes.
  • If the line is too long, shrink the mono size in 2px steps until it fits with 0.55 x h of right padding. Never wrap to a second line.

Content grammar

  • Metrics are real training data or schedule facts: 8 X 400M · PACE 4:52 · REST 60S, WAKE 05:30 · LIFT 06:15.
  • Gear-tag voice: short, factual, no adjectives, no hype.
  • Numbers stay precise. PACE 4:52, not ABOUT 5.

Companion elements

  • Statement block: Trail Black panel, Sun Bone Barlow Condensed caps, maximum three lines. Use when type carries the asset.
  • Training tag: small Trail Black box, IBM Plex Mono caps. Tags the asset like equipment: TRAINING LOG · WK 23 · TUESDAY.
  • Companions never replace the strip. An asset carries the strip plus at most one companion, never two companions.

Placement rules

  • One strip per asset. Bottom edge only.
  • Application photography for speaking and press stays clean: no strip, no overlay.
  • Never place the strip over a face or over a product label.
