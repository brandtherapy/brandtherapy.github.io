Section A · P2 · Brand Strategy

People.

KC serves high-earning, health-curious, undisciplined women in SoCal who know something has to change but do not know where to start. The primary persona is Alina.

High-earning women who need the standard
KC is not speaking to women who need more information. She is speaking to women who need a standard they cannot negotiate with.
01
Strategic position

What is being claimed.

The brand's position on this edge, in one plain sentence.

Position

People

KC serves high-earning, health-curious, undisciplined women in SoCal who know something has to change but do not know where to start. The primary persona is Alina.

Compass summary

High-earning women who need the standard

Alina: 35, SoCal, good salary, health-conscious but undisciplined, holistic-leaning, and awkward around cannabis.

02
Offer boundaries

What belongs and what does not.

The filters that keep this strategic choice operational.

Rules in

Belongs

  • High-earning SoCal women.
  • Corporate, banking, Cedars-Sinai-nurse, and similar high-functioning archetypes.
  • Women who are health-curious and holistic-leaning.
  • Women who consume cannabis awkwardly or without a disciplined frame.
  • Special-needs moms as an identity layer, not the primary segment.
  • One bio line for mother of three when relevant.
Rules out

Does not belong

  • Lazy stoner stereotypes.
  • GLP-1 or shortcut seekers.
  • Pink-Barbie feminine wellness.
  • Infantilized mom content.
  • Public use of her son's face.
  • Treating autism moms as the front of the brand.
03
Tradeoffs

What this choice costs.

Real strategy costs something. If this section is empty, the strategy is performative.

Cost

More precision around Alina, less broad wellness appeal.

Cost

More standard-setting, less soothing.

Cost

More adult feminine power, less cute community language.

04
Application

How this changes behavior.

Strategic choices that don't change behavior are descriptions, not strategy.

Strategy

speak to women who can afford wellness but have not built discipline.

Copy

name the behavior gap without humiliating the woman.

Visuals

show capable, high-standard women in fitness, dinner, and performance spaces.

Sales

make the room feel selective through behavior, not status snobbery.

Content

connect discipline to cognitive longevity, hormonal health, physical health, and daily proof.

05
Proof required

What evidence supports this.

Every public claim traces to verifiable evidence.

  • Persona notes for Alina.
  • Geographic note: SoCal, not Inland Empire and not LA-only.
  • Source statement that special-needs moms are an identity layer only.
  • Approved bio direction before public use.
06
Output control

Rules for AI.

Directives any LLM must follow when producing content for this brand.

  • Keep Alina capable. The issue is undisciplined health behavior, not incompetence.
  • Do not make the brand mommy-first.
  • Do not use her son's image or turn autism into a selling story.
  • Avoid pink, princess, cutesy, or therapy-soft femininity.
