Use this OS with AI

The brand runs on one file

Everything in this OS compiles into one file, ai-context.md. Paste it into ChatGPT or Claude and the AI writes on strategy, in your voice, under your channel rules. No file, no standard.

Open the file
AI-CONTEXT.MD10,900 WORDS~21K TOKENSONE PASTEEVERY CHANNEL

The loop

Ten minutes from sitting down to a reviewable draft. Same loop every time.

01

Open

Open ChatGPT or Claude. Start a new chat. One chat per task. Long chats drift off voice.

02

Paste

Hit Copy the brain above and paste it as your first message. Send it before you ask for anything.

03

Ask

Say what you need in one line. Channel, topic, goal. Or grab a starter prompt below.

Starter prompts

Copy one, replace the brackets, send. Each prompt names its channel so the right rules load.

Instagram

Training-log post

Write an Instagram training-log post.

What happened: [today's session in one or two lines. What you did. Where it got hard.]

Follow the Instagram channel rules in this context. Caption with a one-line hook up front. This is a draft for my review, not a final post.
Email

Weekly email

Draft this week's email.

This week's theme: [one line]
Proof from my own week: [what you actually did, or skipped, and what it cost]

Follow the email channel rules in this context. Subject line, short sections, one clear next action for the reader. Draft only. I approve before anything sends.
Podcast

Episode promo

Write the promo kit for a new episode.

Episode: [title or topic]
The moment worth repeating: [the line or story that earns the listen]

Give me one Instagram caption, one email blurb, and three short hooks. Follow each channel's rules in this context. Drafts only.

The standard still applies

  • The AI drafts. KC approves. Nothing publishes straight from a chat.
  • Names, numbers, dates, and health claims get checked against reality before anything goes out.
  • If the output sounds like a generic coach, the file was not pasted. Back to step one.
  • When the file changes, recopy it. Old paste, old brand.
