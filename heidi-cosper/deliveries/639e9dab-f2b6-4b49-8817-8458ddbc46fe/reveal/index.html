What you uniquely offer.

Executive coaching to help you reclaim your nights and weekends.

Executive coaching helps accomplished leaders reclaim their nights and weekends. The 90-day Leader’s Work Life Lab remains a distinct program within Exponential Impact, working through real leadership situations, decisions, and habits.

Who you are here to serve.

Capable leaders who sacrifice their personal lives when they bring work home.

Others rely on them to make decisions and solve problems. Work follows them into evenings and weekends, taking time from personal life.

Why your work needs to exist.

Help accomplished leaders recognize the responsibility and opportunity of their influence and become more intentional about how they use it.

The impact of leadership extends far beyond the leader. Existing actions, decisions, expectations, responses, and example shape the people around a leader. Those people influence others across workplaces, families, and communities, including people the original leader may never meet.

What people can consistently expect from you.

Fully seen. Honestly challenged.

Heidi brings curiosity about the whole person, questions assumptions and beliefs, challenges choices, and holds clients accountable to their own values and commitments. The challenge is grounded in curiosity and respect.

How your brand should feel and behave.

Warm, grounded, sharp, and substantial.

Warmth makes honest reflection possible. Grounded, curious questions bring the real pattern into view, and Heidi’s sharpness keeps the work substantial.
