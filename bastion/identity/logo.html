03
Clear space

One seat of room on every side

Keep an empty margin as tall as the seat square around the logo. Nothing else enters it.

The margin equals the height of the seat square on every side.

In the horizontal file that margin is already built in around the artwork.

04
Size

Smallest useful size

Below these sizes the open C and the seat stop reading clearly.

Horizontal logo in print: 38 mm wide.

Horizontal logo on screen: 160 px wide.

Mark on screen: 16 px, using the 16 px file.

06
Misuse

What breaks the mark

These are the failures that make the mark unrecognizable or off-brand.

Never

Never recolor the seat outside Brick Red on light and Brick Red Light on dark.Never separate the seat from the C or animate it out of the letter.Never set BASTION COMPANIES in a font other than Fjalla One.Never place the logo on a photograph without a solid Harbor Navy or Ivory field behind it.Never stretch, outline, shadow or rotate the lockups.
