Voice

How the brand speaks. Principles first, then what's banned, then exemplars.

01
Operating rules

Voice principles

See STRATEGY.md Voice. State the role, the number and the source.

Principles

  • State the role, the number and the source.
  • Short declarative sentences in a civic register.
  • Credit partners by name.

On-voice examples

  • "A seat at the table." It names the purpose in five plain words.
  • "Built in from day one." It states the Promise as a fact about the deal.
  • "Partners on every project." It credits the people Bastion brings in.

Off-voice traps

  • "We're changing the game." It imports a football metaphor and promises nothing specific.
  • "Giving back to the community." It frames ownership as charity.
