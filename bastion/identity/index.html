08
System · Constraints

Design Rules

Non-negotiable constraints. These rules exist to maintain visual coherence across every touchpoint.

01
The seat is the only accent

Brick Red appears once per surface as a solid square, a short rule or one key figure. Never as a background, gradient or tint.

02
Numbers carry the argument

Lead with a figure, then its plain-language meaning, then its source in Granite. Every figure keeps its source line.

03
Square, flush and load-bearing

Hard edges, flush-left text, full-bleed bands and a visible grid. No rounded cards, glows, shadows or rotations.

04
Name the partners

Partner names and project roles sit in tracked Fjalla labels. Bastion never takes credit beyond its role.

05
Separate from Gosder's personal brand

No Cobalt fields, Saddle Leather panels, serif accents, lifestyle imagery or football references. Gosder appears as founder, in his own section.
