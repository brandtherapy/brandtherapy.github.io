02
Type system

Typography

Bastion type system: Fjalla One carries editorial warmth and major brand headlines; Public Sans carries navigation, body text, labels, forms, and practical service detail. The renderer must not fall back to FP typography.

Editorial display
Fjalla One

Use: brand headlines, expressive lines, source-board phrases, footer promise, and warm high-touch moments.

Editorial echo
Fjalla One

Use: mixed-headline emphasis and large pull moments. Keep it elegant and spacious, not ornate.

Functional sans
Public Sans

Use: body text, form fields, small proof lines, and practical advisory copy. Keep it plain, legible, and human.
