Section A · P2 · Brand Strategy

People

Bastion speaks first to the lead developers and public landowners who choose a co-developer, and second to the minority-owned firms and investors it brings in.

Developers and public landowners who need minority participation that holds up, and the firms ready to do the work
Persona: The Lead Developer on public land. They are assembling a team for a large project where the landowner scores diversity, inclusion and community benefit. They need a co-developer whose commitments are documented and written into the deal. Without one, minority participation becomes a compliance line and fades after the award. The trigger is a request for proposals or a joint venture that asks who owns the project and who gets the contracts.

Rules in

Belongs

  • Lead developers, capital partners and public agencies such as Massport.
  • Minority and women-owned contractors, designers, operators and investors ready for larger work.
  • Community stakeholders who judge whether the commitments are real.
Rules out

Does not belong

  • Addressing minority-owned firms as charity recipients.
  • Copy that sounds like a grant application or a pitch for sympathy.
