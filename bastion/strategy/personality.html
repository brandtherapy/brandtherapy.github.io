Personality

Main Brand Archetype: Ruler, balanced by Everyman. Bastion carries itself like an institution that keeps its word: composed, precise about numbers and roles, and plainly rooted in Boston neighborhoods.

Steady, exact and civic
Gosder's personal brand is Hero with Sage and Explorer and promises Strive and savor. Bastion is Ruler with Everyman and promises Built in from day one. The company never borrows his voice, his Promise or his personal imagery.
