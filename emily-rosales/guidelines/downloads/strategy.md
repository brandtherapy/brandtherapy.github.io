# Strategy | Emily Rosales

Emily helps established consultants and advisors clarify their positioning, shape their message, and align their digital presence. The work is for excellent service professionals whose public position no longer reflects the business they have built.

## Focus Star

- **Product:** Positioning and messaging strategy for an aligned digital presence.
- **People:** Established consultants and advisors whose positioning has gone stale.
- **Purpose:** Help the right clients understand, trust, and choose them for their real value.
- **Promise:** Clarity before visibility.
- **Personality:** Quiet confidence and honest guidance.

The useful sequence is Foundation, Visibility, Optimization. First clarify the audience, value, and message. Bring that position into priority touchpoints. Review what is resonating and refine.
