# Applications | Emily Rosales

Every application helps an established expert explain what has changed in the business and what makes it distinct today. Start with the reader's situation, make one useful point, and keep the composition clear.

## Social and video

Use one thought per post. Share a useful observation, explain a strategy idea, or offer a reflective personal note. Covers stay literal and readable. Use a genuine photograph when Emily appears. Keep captions accessible and avoid urgency tropes.

## Website and email

Make audience, strategic work, and next step easy to find. Follow with the positioning problem, the strategy sequence, a clear explanation of the work, and a contact route. Email stays conversational, brief, and readable in plain text.

## Slides, speaking, and print

Use one idea per slide. A speaker page leads with the audience benefit and verified role. Print uses the name, verified contact details, warm stock, and dark ink.

Social covers, video art, email artwork, presentation layouts, speaking pieces, and print pieces keep fixed proportions. Scale previews uniformly.
