# Emily Rosales brand context

Use this context to create new materials for Emily Rosales and Simply.Me Coaching. Strategy leads. Follow the visual identity, relevant application rules, and channel guide.

## Strategy

Emily helps established consultants and advisors clarify positioning, shape messaging, and align their digital presence. Her central audience is excellent at the work and often referral-led, while the public message still describes an earlier version of the business. The useful sequence is Foundation, Visibility, Optimization.

Purpose: help the right clients understand, trust, and choose excellent service professionals for their real value.

Promise: clarity before visibility. Start with what sets the client apart. Give honest guidance, including when an observation is uncomfortable.

Personality: quiet confidence and honest guidance. Warm, polished, direct, calm, precise, and thoughtful. Make complex ideas feel simple without talking down to the reader.

## Visual identity

- Deep navy #142939 anchors the system.
- Warm ivory #F5F0E6 is the main canvas.
- Warm muted sand softens secondary surfaces.
- Sea-glass #91AEA6 is a restrained accent.
- Fraunces headlines, Roboto body and utility type.
- The Clear Edge is a broad luminous diagonal translucent ivory plane across navy, resolving a focused area. Use the supplied raster asset. It can bleed off-canvas. Keep it away from faces and important text.
- Portraits should feel natural, composed, softly lit, and recognizably Emily. Use genuine photographs of Emily.

## Writing and applications

Start with the reader's situation, then make one useful point. Use complete, plain sentences and specific evidence. Avoid hype, fake urgency, guru language, status props, unsupported client claims, and visibility guarantees. A natural personal reflection can add perspective while the reader's business remains central.

Respect each target format and safe area. Social covers, video art, slides, email artwork, speaking pieces, and print layouts should scale uniformly in previews. Check copy, contrast, crop, readability, and factual details.

## Factual limits

Do not invent client outcomes, testimonials, credentials, event bookings, contact information, prices, dates, or availability. Verify these from current supplied sources. Do not treat social engagement counts as business proof.
