# Channel guide | Emily Rosales

The voice is warm, polished, direct, calm, precise, and honest. Start with the reader's situation. Connect one observation to a practical choice. Let confidence come from specific language and grounded evidence.

- **Social:** Help established consultants and advisors recognize when their positioning no longer reflects their work.
- **Video:** Explain one observation to one thoughtful person. Keep the pace conversational.
- **Website:** Make the audience, strategic work, and next step clear.
- **Email:** Use a plain subject, short paragraphs, and one purpose.
- **Slides:** Use spoken-language headlines and one idea per slide.
- **Speaking:** Make the audience takeaway concrete. Use verified event facts.
- **Print:** Prioritize legibility, accurate contact information, and restrained materials.

Avoid hype, fake urgency, unsupported client transformations, and outcome guarantees. Verify current offers, credentials, event details, contact details, and prices before use.
