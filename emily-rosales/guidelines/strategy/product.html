P1 / Product

Positioning and messaging strategy for an aligned digital presence.

Emily helps established consultants and advisors clarify positioning, shape messaging, and align their digital presence. The work begins with strategic clarity. Visibility follows when the meaning is clear.

On this page
  1. What it means
  2. Do and avoid
  3. Put it to work

01

What it means

Name the value first. The message and the places people meet the business follow from it.

02

Do and avoid

Do

  • Lead with positioning and messaging
  • Show the sequence: strategy, then messaging, then digital presence
  • Describe the outcome as a clearer business to understand

Avoid

  • Selling design production, logo work, or content management
  • Promising reach as the result
  • Listing services without the strategic reason behind them

03

Put it to work

  • Website headlines name the value before the service list
  • Offers describe the decision they help the client make
  • Every piece connects back to positioning
