P3 / Purpose

Help excellent service professionals get clear on their value so the right clients can understand, trust, and choose them.

Excellent service professionals deserve to be chosen for their real value. The work makes that value clear enough for the right clients to understand, trust, and choose them.

On this page
  1. What it means
  2. Do and avoid
  3. Put it to work

01

What it means

Every piece should make the right client's choice easier.

02

Do and avoid

Do

  • Connect clarity to being chosen by the right clients
  • Show why the work matters to the reader's business
  • Keep the right fit in view, not the largest audience

Avoid

  • Visibility for its own sake
  • Vanity metrics as proof of value
  • Chasing every possible client

03

Put it to work

  • Calls to action invite a fit conversation
  • Content explains how clarity changes a buying decision
  • Proof stays honest and specific
