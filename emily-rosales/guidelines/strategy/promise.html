04

P4 / Promise

Clarity before visibility.

Start with what sets the client apart and give honest guidance, including when the observation is uncomfortable. More reach never stands in for a clear position.

On this page
  1. What it means
  2. Do and avoid
  3. Put it to work

01

What it means

Clarity comes first. Visibility is useful only once the meaning is clear.

02

Do and avoid

Do

  • Give honest, specific guidance
  • Sequence the work: foundation, visibility, optimization
  • Make one clear point per piece

Avoid

  • Recommending more posting as the fix
  • Guarantees of clients, reach, or results
  • Softening an observation until it says nothing

03

Put it to work

  • Content teaches the order of the work
  • Offers start with the foundation
  • Headlines carry one clear idea
