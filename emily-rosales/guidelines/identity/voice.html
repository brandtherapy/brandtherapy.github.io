Section B

Voice and Writing

Say what matters with warmth and precision.

On this page
  1. Tone
  2. Voice rules
  3. On-voice lines
  4. Avoid

01

Tone

A thoughtful guide, speaking plainly.

Warm

Kind and human.

Polished

Considered and clean.

Direct

Says the point plainly.

Honest

Names what matters, even when uncomfortable.

02

Voice rules

Start with the reader's situation

Open with what the reader is facing before offering a view.

Make one useful point

Each piece carries a single idea the reader can use.

Keep confidence grounded

Speak from experience without hype or guarantees.

Let Emily be present

Write as a thoughtful guide speaking plainly.

03

On-voice lines

  • Your expertise doesn't position itself.
  • A clear message travels further.
  • Does your message describe the business you run today?

04

Avoid

  • Hype, urgency, and influencer cadence
  • Jargon that hides the point
  • Promises of reach, clients, or results
