How Emily shows up

02

Wardrobe

Ivory and soft neutrals

The default for warm, open portraits and editorial pages.

Navy

For focused, professional moments such as speaking and title frames.

03

Photography

Do

  • Soft daylight and an uncluttered background
  • Wardrobe in navy, cream, muted blue, or natural neutrals
  • A working portrait when she is listening, reading, writing, or considering a message

Avoid

  • Staged pointing and status props
  • Heavy retouching
  • Text over the face
