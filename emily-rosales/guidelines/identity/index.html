01

Creative Direction

Direction

A calm coastal editorial world, with deep navy, warm ivory, elegant serif headlines, and a strong portrait presence.

The idea

A composed identity with a clear focal point. Every layout makes one idea easier to see.

How it should feel

Editorial, warm, precise, and quiet.

What to avoid

Hype, clutter, luxury cliches, and staged energy.

04

Keep it consistent

See Look and Feel, Brand Signature, Voice and Writing, and Personal Branding for exact rules.

Always do

  • Keep one idea in focus per layout
  • Let navy ground and ivory open the composition
  • Show Emily present and at ease

Avoid

  • Busy layouts and stacked messages
  • Bright accents beyond a restrained sea-glass cue
  • Staged poses and status props
