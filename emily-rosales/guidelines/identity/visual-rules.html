How the brand is tuned

Each line shows where the brand sits between two qualities.

Eleven scales keep the brand in character across pages, posts, and conversations. The percentage is how strongly the brand leans to that side. The strongest settings are the ones to protect.

Lean into

  • Calm
  • Confident
  • Decisive

  • Down-to-earth
  • Honest
  • Insightful
  • Knowledgeable
  • Mature
  • Serious
  • Simple
  • Thoughtful
  • Trustworthy

Move away from

  • Hype
  • Urgency
  • Oversized claims
  • Status props

Three scales stay open: Friendly or Professional, Innovative or Traditional, Urban or Earthy.

02

Brand personality

The personality mix behind every visual and verbal choice.

Sage leads the brand. Ruler, Friend and Innocent give it structure, warmth, and simplicity.

  1. Sage, 32%. Helps people understand the world through knowledge, truth, and perspective.
  2. Ruler, 18%. Creates confidence through leadership, standards, control, and dependable order.
  3. Friend, 15%. Creates belonging through familiarity, equality, and everyday trust.
  4. Innocent, 15%. Makes life feel simpler and safer through optimism, honesty, and clarity.

Hero, Caregiver, Magician, and Lover appear as lighter traces.

Visual rules
