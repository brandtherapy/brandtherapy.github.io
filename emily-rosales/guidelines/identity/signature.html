01

The Clear Edge

A broad luminous diagonal plane crosses a diffuse navy field and resolves one focused area. It carries clarity through light, contrast, and composition.

02

Usage

Use it for

  • Section openers and title fields
  • Portrait and editorial image transitions
  • Occasional social covers and presentation dividers

Keep it clear

  • Turning it into an outline, icon, or drawn logo
  • Covering the subject or the message
  • Shrinking it inside the canvas instead of letting it crop beyond
