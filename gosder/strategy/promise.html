P4 / Promise

Strive and savor

Strive and savor means working with discipline while making room to live fully.

On this page
  1. What it means
  2. Do and avoid
  3. Put it to work

01

What it means

Work with devotion. Enjoy without guilt. Keep responsibility and pleasure in balance.

02

Do and avoid

Do

  • Preparation, repetition, standards, resilience, and disciplined action.
  • Recovery, meditation, faith, family, relationships, travel, culture, food, art, music, golf, fishing, and responsible pleasure.
  • Products, practices, and environments that help serious people participate in life with clarity and intention.
  • Celebrating progress without pretending the work was easy.

Avoid

  • Grind culture, performative suffering, punishment, and pain as identity.
  • Pleasure framed as escape from responsibility or consumption as a substitute for meaning.
  • Guilt around rest, recovery, celebration, or responsible enjoyment.
  • Balance framed as passivity, softness, lower standards, or lack of ambition.

03

Put it to work

  • Strategy: Accept opportunities that honor both meaningful achievement and the life surrounding it.
  • Copy: Pair the work with what the work makes possible. Never show striving without savoring or enjoyment without responsibility.
  • Visuals: Hold effort and ease in the same frame: training and table, build site and journey, stone and warm light.
  • Sales: Evaluate partners, products, and ventures by whether they support disciplined progress and responsible enjoyment.
