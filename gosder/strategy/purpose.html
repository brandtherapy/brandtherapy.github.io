P3 / Purpose

To create the conditions for more people to achieve and live fully

Gosder uses experience, access, mentorship, community, and practical support to help more people move forward.

On this page
  1. What it means
  2. Do and avoid
  3. Put it to work

01

What it means

Open doors, share what he has learned, provide practical support, and leave people better equipped.

02

Do and avoid

Do

  • Mentorship, coaching, camps, education, introductions, and access to capable rooms.
  • Haiti work tied to water, health infrastructure, family history, and specific community needs.
  • Minority economic inclusion, jobs, ownership, investment, and participation in development.
  • Ventures that improve access to responsible products, opportunity, community, or wellbeing.

Avoid

  • Generic claims about helping people without a concrete condition, opportunity, or outcome.
  • Savior framing, charity spectacle, or treating communities as proof of Gosder's goodness.
  • Scattered causes without ownership, follow-through, or verifiable scope.
  • Opportunity language that ignores preparation, standards, responsibility, or structural barriers.

03

Put it to work

  • Strategy: Prioritize initiatives that change access, capability, infrastructure, opportunity, or belonging in measurable ways.
  • Copy: Name the missing condition, what is being built, who owns the work, and what becomes possible.
  • Visuals: Center the people, place, tools, teams, and progress rather than ceremonial charity imagery.
  • Sales: Choose partners who contribute resources, expertise, access, or execution rather than borrowing the mission for reputation.
