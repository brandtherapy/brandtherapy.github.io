P1 / Product

An inspiring example of resilience, devotion, and balance

Gosder's personal brand is the example itself: a life where resilience, devotion, and balance are practiced, not preached.

On this page
  1. What it means
  2. Do and avoid
  3. Put it to work

01

What it means

Show the practice, the work, and the life it makes possible.

02

Do and avoid

Do

  • Real examples from football, entrepreneurship, mentorship, faith, training, recovery, reading, meditation, travel, cooking, music, golf, and fishing.
  • Honest accounts of preparation, setbacks, repetition, responsibility, and the choices required to keep going.
  • Ventures and community work used as evidence of the operating philosophy, not as separate brands competing for attention.
  • Practical lessons that help people apply the example to their own lives.

Avoid

  • Making Primitiv, football, cannabis, Bastion, or any single venture the whole product.
  • Generic motivation, resilience slogans, or advice unsupported by real examples.
  • Pain worship, punishment, and performative toughness.
  • Positioning Gosder as a health influencer, productivity guru, or celebrity cannabis mascot.

03

Put it to work

  • Strategy: Organize the personal brand above the ventures, using each venture as proof of the same way of living.
  • Copy: Start with a real choice, practice, obstacle, or pleasure and name what it demonstrates.
  • Visuals: Show Gosder doing, building, learning, recovering, serving, and enjoying. Avoid staged flexing or generic peak imagery.
  • Sales: Frame speaking, mentorship, partnerships, and content around access to a lived operating philosophy, not celebrity proximity.
