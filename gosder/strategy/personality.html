P5 / Personality

A stoic Renaissance man with an Epicurean appetite for life

Gosder shows up with calm strength, curiosity, good judgment, warmth, and an appetite for life.

On this page
  1. What it means
  2. Do and avoid
  3. Put it to work

01

What it means

Be calm, curious, cultured, and direct.

02

Do and avoid

Do

  • Humble, selfless, determined, resilient, serious, generous, and mature.
  • Stoic composure, emotional self-command, patience, listening, and quiet confidence.
  • Renaissance breadth across business, sport, faith, culture, languages, travel, cooking, piano, gardening, boxing, golf, fishing, and reading.
  • Understated luxury, direct communication, playful warmth, and a measured masculine presence open to women and families.

Avoid

  • David Goggins-style pain worship, shouting, intimidation, or punishment as personality.
  • Emotional emptiness, coldness, contempt, or pretending nothing can matter.
  • Loud self-promotion, luxury logos, attention chasing, or workout-selfie performance.
  • Generic wellness influencer, motivational athlete, or celebrity cannabis archetypes.

03

Put it to work

  • Strategy: Let Strive and savor organize the range so every interest demonstrates the same character rather than fragmenting the brand.
  • Copy: Use direct, calm sentences, specific observations, earned advice, dry warmth, and cultural curiosity.
  • Visuals: Combine calm strength with warm evidence of life. Avoid literal fortress, lighthouse, mountain, or summit logos before Creative Direction.
  • Sales: Lead through listening, standards, credibility, and fit rather than dominance or celebrity.
