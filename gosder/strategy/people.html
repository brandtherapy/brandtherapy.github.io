P2 / People

Driven people looking for a tribe where ambition and enjoyment belong together

The brand is for driven people carrying real pressure who want capable peers and room to enjoy what they are building.

On this page
  1. What it means
  2. Do and avoid
  3. Put it to work

01

What it means

Speak to people who work hard, carry responsibility, and want capable peers.

02

Do and avoid

Do

  • Entrepreneurs, athletes, former athletes, builders, leaders, creators, and committed individuals.
  • People whose ambition creates real pressure, expectations, and responsibility.
  • Doers who respect preparation, consistency, faith, learning, and follow-through.
  • People seeking a tribe of peers who understand both the cost and pleasure of achievement.

Avoid

  • Narrowing the audience to athletes, men, cannabis consumers, or wealthy founders.
  • Hustle culture that treats exhaustion as proof of character.
  • Passive inspiration seekers unwilling to practice or act.
  • Audiences looking for noise, shortcuts, status display, or borrowed motivation.

03

Put it to work

  • Strategy: Build a community and content system around shared practice, responsibility, access, and enjoyment rather than making everything about Gosder.
  • Copy: Address capable adults. Name the guilt, loneliness, and tension without treating ambition as a disorder.
  • Visuals: Show peers, teams, families, collaborators, camps, tables, travel, and workspaces alongside solitary discipline.
  • Sales: Focus on people ready to participate, contribute, and learn.
