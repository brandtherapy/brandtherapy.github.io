03

Keep it consistent

Use

  • One clear idea per post
  • Real facts and examples
  • A headline that works without the caption
  • The same visual system across both platforms

Avoid

  • Invented results
  • Generic motivational filler
  • Too many ideas in one post
  • Changing the type, colors, or spacing
