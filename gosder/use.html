AI workflow

Use with AI

Give AI the right brand context, then review the result before using it.

On this page
  1. How it works
  2. Ready-to-use prompts
  3. Final check

01

How it works

Choose one task, give AI the right pages, and review the result.

  1. 01
    Choose the task

    Name the audience, format, and result you need.

  2. 02
    Open the right pages

    Use only the Strategy, Identity, and Application guidance needed for the task.

  3. 03
    Ask for a draft

    Tell AI to follow the brand and ask when information is missing.

  4. 04
    Check the result

    Confirm the facts, voice, images, links, and intended audience.

02

Ready-to-use prompts

Copy a prompt, replace the brackets, and review the result.

LinkedIn or Instagram

Social post

Brand Therapy OS: https://brandtherapy.florianp.com/gosder/
AI context: https://brandtherapy.florianp.com/gosder/ai-context.md

Use the attached Gosder Brand Therapy OS context as your guide.

Create a [LinkedIn or Instagram] draft using the [point of view, diagnosis, framework, proof, or invitation] template.
Topic: [topic]
Real source or example: [real story, fact, or observation]
Desired action: [what the reader should understand or do]

Keep Strive and savor visible through the idea without repeating it mechanically. Do not invent facts, outcomes, or personal history. Return a draft.

Email

Email

Brand Therapy OS: https://brandtherapy.florianp.com/gosder/
AI context: https://brandtherapy.florianp.com/gosder/ai-context.md

Use the attached Gosder Brand Therapy OS context as your guide.

Draft a [field note, invitation, announcement, or personal follow-up].
Audience: [recipient or group]
Real scene or reason for writing: [source]
Main point: [one useful idea]
Next action: [one clear action]

Return subject, preview text, body, and link text. Do not invent contact details or claims. Draft only. Review it before sending.

Website

Website page

Brand Therapy OS: https://brandtherapy.florianp.com/gosder/
AI context: https://brandtherapy.florianp.com/gosder/ai-context.md

Use the attached Gosder Brand Therapy OS context as your guide.

Draft the [Home, About Gosder, or Speaking and Partnerships] page.
Audience: [audience]
Page goal: [goal]
Facts and examples available: [proof]
Primary next action: [action]

Return the page hierarchy, headings, concise copy, image needs, proof needs, metadata, and accessibility notes. Ask if information is missing instead of inventing it.

Editorial and PR

Editorial concept

Brand Therapy OS: https://brandtherapy.florianp.com/gosder/
AI context: https://brandtherapy.florianp.com/gosder/ai-context.md

Use the attached Gosder Brand Therapy OS context as your guide.

Develop an editorial concept about [topic].
Real source material: [interview, biography, or verified facts]
Intended reader: [reader]
Format: [fictional cover, article outline, or PR story angle]

Use a fictional or Gosder-owned masthead. Never imply a real publication commissioned, published, or endorsed the concept. Mark any fact that needs checking.

03

Final check

  • Use verified facts and current brand files
  • Keep the voice and visual rules consistent
  • Do not invent credentials, results, quotes, or endorsements
  • Check every image, face, link, and claim before using it
