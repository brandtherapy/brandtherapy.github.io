Section B

Voice and Writing

Write with calm authority and keep discipline and enjoyment together.

On this page
  1. Core brand copy
  2. Four voice rules
  3. Examples that sound right
  4. Before and after

01

Core brand copy

Tagline

Strive and savor

One-line description

A former NFL tackle and entrepreneur showing driven people how discipline, achievement, and enjoyment can belong in one full life.

Short description

Gosder Cherilus shares a lived operating philosophy for doers who want ambition, resilience, belonging, and enjoyment to strengthen one another.

Short bio

Gosder Cherilus is a former NFL tackle and entrepreneur whose work spans business, mentorship, community, and a disciplined commitment to living fully.

02

Four voice rules

Start with real life

Begin with a real choice, scene, practice, obstacle, or pleasure before naming the principle.

Avoid: Generic motivation or borrowed authority.

Calm and direct

Speak to capable adults in short, complete statements with enough context to be useful.

Avoid: Shouting, performance, or false urgency.

Hold ambition and enjoyment together

Keep standards and enjoyment inside the same idea.

Avoid: Glorifying pain, guilt, or careless indulgence.

Make the message useful to others

Show access, preparation, and what someone can do next.

Avoid: Making Gosder the only hero or using status for its own sake.

03

Examples that sound right

“Ambition can build the life and still crowd out living it.”
“Do the work. Make the turn. Enjoy what the work made possible.”
“Show the work, the handoff, and who carries it next.”
“Bring the work. Leave room for life.”
“Tell me what you are building, what the pressure is costing, and what a fuller outcome would make possible.”

04

Before and after

Instead of

Champions never complain.

Use: Pressure is real. Discipline is what you choose inside it.

Instead of

Outwork everyone.

Use: Do the work. Then enjoy what the work made possible.

Instead of

Success is the only option.

Use: Choose something worth the effort, prepare fully, and leave room to live along the way.
