Use with AI

Give AI the right brand context, then review the result before using it.

On this page
  1. How it works
  2. Starter prompts
  3. Final check

01

How it works

  1. 01
    Open

    Start a new chat for one task. Long chats drift off voice and rules.

  2. 02
    Paste

    Give the AI the brand context first, before you ask for anything.

  3. 03
    Ask

    Name the channel, format (Finding, Handoff, or Operator Note), topic, and goal.

  4. 04
    Review

    Check facts, voice, images, and The Seam behavior before using anything.

02

Starter prompts

LinkedIn

LinkedIn Finding

Brand Therapy OS: https://brandtherapy.florianp.com/michael-couper/os/
AI context: https://brandtherapy.florianp.com/michael-couper/os/ai-context.md

Use the attached Michael Couper Brand Therapy OS context as fixed guidance.

Draft a LinkedIn Finding post.
Observation: [what I noticed in a conversation, rehearsal, project, or public debate]
What became clearer: [the sharper question or judgment]
Desired reader action: [think differently, ask a better question, or decide with more context]

Follow the LinkedIn Finding rules. Open with the question before the answer. Short paragraphs. No certainty theater, guru framing, or invented credentials. Draft only for human review.

LinkedIn

LinkedIn Handoff

Brand Therapy OS: https://brandtherapy.florianp.com/michael-couper/os/
AI context: https://brandtherapy.florianp.com/michael-couper/os/ai-context.md

Use the attached Michael Couper Brand Therapy OS context as fixed guidance.

Draft a LinkedIn Handoff post that moves one idea from insight to next step.
The finding already made: [one clear judgment]
What to hand off: [the decision, question, or action the reader should take next]
Audience: [independent thinkers facing a consequential choice]

Keep Counterpoint voice. Separate what is known from what is assumed when useful. Do not invent outcomes. Draft only.

LinkedIn Operator Note

Brand Therapy OS: https://brandtherapy.florianp.com/michael-couper/os/
AI context: https://brandtherapy.florianp.com/michael-couper/os/ai-context.md

Use the attached Michael Couper Brand Therapy OS context as fixed guidance.

Draft a LinkedIn Operator Note.
Working context: [studio, desk, rehearsal, teaching, or advisory moment]
Practical note: [one useful operating observation]
Why it matters now: [one sentence]

Keep it calm, exact, and human. No consultant jargon. Draft only for human review.

Instagram

Instagram Finding

Use the attached Michael Couper Brand Therapy OS context as fixed guidance.

Draft an Instagram Finding caption for a 1080x1350 Counterpoint frame.

Closing judgment: [one clear line]

Follow Instagram channel rules. Open with the question. Keep The Seam as one thin glass edge in any visual brief, never a logo. Draft only.

03

Final check

  • Use verified facts and current brand pages
  • Keep voice and visual rules consistent
  • Do not invent credentials, results, quotes, or endorsements
  • Never use the temporary stylescape as a face reference; use the approved face canon and canon stylescape only
