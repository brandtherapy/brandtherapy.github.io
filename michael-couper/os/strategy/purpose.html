Section A · P3 · Brand Strategy

Purpose

Michael's larger purpose is progress through informed human choice. Society cannot move forward when decisions are driven by misinformation, manipulation, tribal certainty, or automated conclusions that remove judgment from the person making the choice.

To move society forward through better-informed human choices
Better information matters because choices have consequences. The goal is not truth as an intellectual trophy, but fuller understanding that improves what people build, protect, and pass forward.
01
Strategic position

What is being claimed

The brand's position on this edge, in one plain sentence.

Position

Purpose

Michael's larger purpose is progress through informed human choice. Society cannot move forward when decisions are driven by misinformation, manipulation, tribal certainty, or automated conclusions that remove judgment from the person making the choice.

Compass summary

To move society forward through better-informed human choices

Put more truth, context, creativity, and human judgment into the choices that shape work, culture, technology, and civic life.

02
Offer boundaries

What belongs and what does not

The filters that keep this strategic choice operational.

Rules in

Belongs

  • Evidence, multiple perspectives, and context before conclusion.
  • Human creativity and judgment as essential forces in progress.
  • Decisions that balance technical possibility with cultural and human consequence.
  • Art, education, business, technology, and civic life as connected fields of progress.
Rules out

Does not belong

  • Truth-seeking as debate sport or personal superiority.
  • Misinformation, marketing spin, and ideological certainty replacing evidence.
  • Anti-AI panic or blind AI deference.
  • False balance that treats every claim as equally supported.
  • Grand statements about humanity without a real decision underneath them.
03
Tradeoffs

What this choice costs

Real strategy costs something. If this section is empty, the strategy is performative.

Cost

More ambitious societal purpose, greater need to ground every public claim in specific choices and examples.

Cost

More defense of human judgment, less room for convenient automation or ideological shortcuts.

Cost

More willingness to hold competing perspectives, less ability to satisfy audiences demanding immediate allegiance.

04
Application

How this changes behavior

Strategic choices that don't change behavior are descriptions, not strategy.

Strategy

Prioritize work where better-informed choice can affect culture, business, technology, education, or community.

Copy

Connect truth and context to the human consequence of the decision.

Visuals

Show evidence and imagination in the same world, never humans against machines as a simplistic battle.

Sales

Frame the value around improved judgment, not the performance of expertise.

Content

Examine decisions where facts, assumptions, incentives, creativity, and human stakes collide.

05
Proof required

What evidence supports this

Every public claim traces to verifiable evidence.

  • Michael's stated commitment to human creativity and work that enriches lives.
  • His civic example of balancing personal preference against the needs of the community.
  • Examples where evidence changed or complicated his original position.
  • Clear separation between sourced fact, interpretation, and personal judgment.
06
Output control

Rules for AI

Directives any LLM must follow when producing content for this brand.

  • Never frame Michael as the owner of the truth.
  • Make the human consequence of better information visible.
  • Do not invent consensus, evidence, or societal outcomes.
  • Distinguish evidence, inference, intuition, and opinion explicitly when the distinction matters.
