Section A · P1 · Brand Strategy

Product

The product is not one profession, company, or opinion. It is Michael's ability to connect evidence, systems, art, business, technology, and civic reality so a difficult choice can be understood in full.

The context and tools to see the whole picture
Michael's rare value is not that he has many interests. It is that he can move between disciplines, expose assumptions, and turn a wider field of evidence into better judgment.
01
Strategic position

What is being claimed

The brand's position on this edge, in one plain sentence.

Position

Product

The product is not one profession, company, or opinion. It is Michael's ability to connect evidence, systems, art, business, technology, and civic reality so a difficult choice can be understood in full.

Compass summary

The context and tools to see the whole picture

Bring the missing context into view. Make complexity usable without flattening it into an automatic answer.

02
Offer boundaries

What belongs and what does not

The filters that keep this strategic choice operational.

Rules in

Belongs

  • Cross-disciplinary explanations that connect technology, art, business, science, philosophy, and civic life.
  • Frameworks, tools, and context that help people inspect a decision for themselves.
  • Novel problems where the premise matters as much as the proposed answer.
  • Work that preserves complexity long enough to reveal the real choice.
Rules out

Does not belong

  • Automated answers presented as judgment.
  • Positioning Michael as only a marketer, software developer, composer, founder, or political voice.
  • A list of impressive titles without a useful synthesis.
  • Generic thought leadership, productivity advice, or certainty theater.
  • Complexity used to avoid making a decision.
03
Tradeoffs

What this choice costs

Real strategy costs something. If this section is empty, the strategy is performative.

Cost

More range and originality, less immediate category simplicity.

Cost

More context before judgment, less appetite for instant conclusions.

Cost

More authority from demonstrated synthesis, greater need to show concrete cases where the wider view changed the decision.

04
Application

How this changes behavior

Strategic choices that don't change behavior are descriptions, not strategy.

Strategy

Organize offers and public work around consequential questions, not around Michael's separate job titles.

Copy

Start with the incomplete picture or hidden assumption, then show what becomes visible when more perspectives enter.

Visuals

Make multiple systems or viewpoints legible without turning the work into a technical dashboard.

Sales

Sell the quality of the thinking and the resulting decision, not access to a generic polymath.

Content

Use real questions from technology, art, business, and civic life to demonstrate the method.

05
Proof required

What evidence supports this

Every public claim traces to verifiable evidence.

  • Michael's work across CCS Interactive, Lendrose, software, composition, teaching, and civic decision-making.
  • Examples where he identified a hidden premise or reframed a novel problem.
  • Evidence of tools, systems, or decisions produced from cross-disciplinary thinking.
  • Source meetings: June 25, June 30, and July 8 Brand Therapy sessions.
06
Output control

Rules for AI

Directives any LLM must follow when producing content for this brand.

  • Do not reduce Michael to one profession or describe breadth as the product by itself.
  • Show how the wider view changes a question, choice, or outcome.
  • Never invent business results, political positions, clients, or credentials.
  • Preserve complexity, then land it in a usable decision.
