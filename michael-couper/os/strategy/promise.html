Section A · P4 · Brand Strategy

Promise

Follow the question is the operating commitment. Before accepting an answer, Michael examines the premise, looks for the missing perspective, tests the evidence, and stays with the inquiry until it can support a real judgment.

Follow the question
Inquiry is valuable only when it changes the decision. Follow the question rejects both reflexive answers and curiosity that never lands anywhere.
01
Strategic position

What is being claimed

The brand's position on this edge, in one plain sentence.

Position

Promise

Follow the question is the operating commitment. Before accepting an answer, Michael examines the premise, looks for the missing perspective, tests the evidence, and stays with the inquiry until it can support a real judgment.

Compass summary

Follow the question

Question first. Gather the fuller picture. Let evidence and human judgment close the inquiry into a choice.

02
Offer boundaries

What belongs and what does not

The filters that keep this strategic choice operational.

Rules in

Belongs

  • Question the premise before optimizing the answer.
  • Seek evidence that could change the current view.
  • Bring competing perspectives into the same decision.
  • Admit uncertainty without surrendering judgment.
  • Close the inquiry when the available evidence is sufficient to act responsibly.
Rules out

Does not belong

  • Answers chosen before the question is understood.
  • Contrarianism for attention or identity.
  • Endless curiosity used to avoid commitment.
  • Intuition overriding stronger evidence without acknowledgment.
  • Automated or ideological certainty presented as neutral truth.
03
Tradeoffs

What this choice costs

Real strategy costs something. If this section is empty, the strategy is performative.

Cost

More durable judgment, slower gratification than instant answers.

Cost

More intellectual humility, less performance of certainty.

Cost

More openness to revision, greater responsibility to explain why the inquiry has reached a decision.

04
Application

How this changes behavior

Strategic choices that don't change behavior are descriptions, not strategy.

Strategy

Every offer, topic, and partnership should begin with a consequential question worth following.

Copy

Use questions to open inquiry, then provide context and a clear landing.

Visuals

Show movement from fragmented evidence toward a chosen point without using a generic question-mark motif.

Sales

Diagnose the premise before prescribing a solution.

Content

Reveal what changed once the original question was examined more deeply.

05
Proof required

What evidence supports this

Every public claim traces to verifiable evidence.

  • July 8 workshop evidence around questioning assumptions and seeing perspectives beyond one point of view.
  • Michael's examples of choosing the overlooked objective or challenging the prevailing frame.
  • Published or working examples where inquiry produced a different decision.
06
Output control

Rules for AI

Directives any LLM must follow when producing content for this brand.

  • Keep the exact approved Promise: Follow the question.
  • Keep the nested Brand DNA: Question First.
  • Never turn the Promise into endless curiosity, generic skepticism, or a question-mark slogan.
  • Every inquiry-led output must eventually state what became clearer or what choice follows.
