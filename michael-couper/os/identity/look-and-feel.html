The operational layer of the visual identity. Color, typography, layout, photography, and the categories the brand refuses to look like.

02
Type system

Typography

Archivo Narrow concentrates short, consequential statements. Archivo keeps reasoning calm, legible, and human. The system is exact without becoming academic, orchestral, or technology-coded.

Exact display
Archivo Narrow

Use: short headlines, inquiry prompts, and decisive statements. Keep line lengths controlled and avoid score-sheet mimicry.

Human reasoning
Archivo

Use: explanation, evidence labels, navigation, and long-form thought. Keep the rhythm direct, quiet, and readable.

03
System · Rules

Do & Don't

Non-negotiable. These rules exist to maintain visual coherence across every touchpoint.

Do

Keep rational structure and artistic material depth in visible tension.

Do

Use The Seam as tiny host-aligned purple glass.

Do

Show distinct life contexts: desk, teaching, media. Never reuse the same photo twice.

Do

Prefer no-glasses for heroes; one small glasses frame only.

Do

Use the temporary stylescape as art-direction evidence for Section B.

Don't

Use question marks as symbols or patterns.

Don't

Create professor cosplay or music-only reduction.

Don't

Use rings, discs, Venns, eyes, water pours, or clothing logos.

Don't

Wash scenes in purple mysticism or neon cyberpunk.

Don't

Treat The Seam as a logo.

Don't

Publish, deliver, or use likeness publicly without separate approval.

04
Imagery · Photography

Photography

How real photos and AI-generated imagery should look.

Quality standard: Intimate directional light, dark mineral or bone paper fields, real evidence, teaching or media context, masculine techy Counterpoint geometry. The Seam appears as a tiny glass seam or edge, never a product hero.

Modes:

  • Inquiry at work: Michael with evidence at the desk.
  • Teaching: Conservatory or studio instruction context.
  • Media: Podcast or web templates as supporting frames.

Forbidden imagery: Unapproved public likeness, professor costume, purple smoke, MasterCard discs, labeled Venns, music-only stage performance as the brand center, and glasses on every portrait.

The separate human-gate.json remains the likeness SSOT. Temporary stylescape faces are art-direction only until Stage 5b canon rebuild.
