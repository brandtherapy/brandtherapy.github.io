Section B

Voice and Writing

Write with calm authority and follow the question to a judgment.

On this page
  1. Voice rules
  2. On-voice lines
  3. Avoid

01

Voice rules

Follow the question

Open with the premise before presenting the conclusion.

Show the whole picture

Bring context and competing perspectives without losing the decision.

Separate the layers

Distinguish evidence, inference, intuition, and opinion when it matters.

Serious but playful

Keep serious ideas accessible with direct language and dry wit.

02

On-voice lines

  • Before we argue about the answer, what question are we actually trying to solve?
  • The data matters. So does the context the data cannot hold.
  • Changing your mind is not weakness. It is evidence that the inquiry worked.

03

Avoid

  • Automatic answers and borrowed certainty
  • Sales language, guru framing, and attention-seeking contrarianism
  • Complexity that never lands in a judgment, and mysticism without consequence
