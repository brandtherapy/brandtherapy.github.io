Section B · Brand Identity · Voice

Voice

How the brand speaks. Principles first, then what's banned, then exemplars.

01
Operating rules

Voice principles

Principles

  • Follow the question before presenting the conclusion.
  • Show context and competing perspectives without losing the decision.
  • Separate evidence, inference, intuition, and opinion when the distinction matters.
  • Keep serious ideas accessible through direct language and dry playfulness.

On-Voice Examples

  • "Before we argue about the answer, what question are we actually trying to solve?"
  • "The data matters. So does the context the data cannot hold."
  • "Changing your mind is not weakness. It is evidence that the inquiry worked."

Avoid

  • Automatic answers and borrowed certainty.
  • Sales language, guru framing, and attention-seeking contrarianism.
  • Complexity that never lands in a judgment.
  • Mystical language without intellectual or human consequence.
