Section B

Brand Signature

Use The Seam to keep a quiet purple-glass accent on real structures.

On this page
  1. Master
  2. Usage

01

Master

Translucent purple glass follows a real structure while Michael stays the subject.

02

Usage

  • Follow an existing host line: thread, seam, edge, or frame
  • Keep the translucent purple glass accent small and subtle
  • The Seam adapts to the host form and holds no fixed shape
  • Never treat The Seam as a logo, ring, disc, Venn, or water pour
