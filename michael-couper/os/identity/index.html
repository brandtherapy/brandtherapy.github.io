02
Strategy translation

What this direction is saying

Rational structure holds artistic depth. The world is quiet and luxurious, but never academic, theatrical, or decorative. Translucent color is the one new material force inside it.

Best Fit

Private identity review, long-form public thinking, speaking, publishing, and cross-disciplinary decision work after approval.

Watchout

It can become professor cosplay, generic luxury, purple mysticism, a political badge, a consulting diagram, or a music-only reduction.

Operating Idea

Keep competing perspectives translucent and legible until the evidence supports one responsible judgment, and let the overlap show it.

03
Creative platform

The world this builds

The direction is treated as a brand system, not a palette. These are the decisions that website, content, email, collateral, and social surfaces inherit.

Creative idea

Make Follow the question physical. Two translucent perspectives stay independently legible, and where they genuinely overlap a third color appears that neither field holds alone.

World

Dark mineral surfaces, tactile bone paper, exact evidence layers, intimate directional light, restrained cultural depth, and translucent color as a real material force.

Feeling

Quiet, exact, artistic, serious, cultivated, and independently minded.

Behavior

Keep competing perspectives visible until one overlap event resolves the composition.

Signature line

Follow the question

Not this

Academic costume, generic premium styling, mystical decoration, founder theater, political badging, or a music identity with no wider field.

07
Signature system

Mark and device

Translucent purple glass that adapts to host lines it meets. No fixed shape. Small subtle accents along threads, seams, edges, and frames. Not literal water. Not a logo.

The Union

On a second look, the viewer notices subtle purple glass following a real structure while Michael remains the subject.

Use

  • Tiny adaptive glass along a jacket seam or stitch.
  • Thin edge film on UI or print chrome.
  • Quiet frame accent on podcast or editorial frames.

Avoid

  • rings, discs, Venn, eye geometry
  • water pour sculptures or large glass masses
  • clothing pins, badges, embroidery logos
  • MasterCard equal red/blue discs
  • treating the device as a logo

09
AI production rules

Generator notes

Constraints for private visual studies only. They do not authorize applications, delivery, publication, or public likeness use.

Do

  • Use the separate approved private face canon as the only likeness reference for future production.
  • Treat this board as art-direction evidence, not exact-likeness canon.
  • Preserve Michael's age, dark curly hair, light stubble, natural skin, and body proportions.
  • Use thick rectangular glasses consistently when this outfit route is active.
  • Do not use the rejected accent raster as an image reference.
  • Treat the Overlap signature grammar as a hypothesis until a separate visual proof is approved.
  • Render purple only inside an overlap of the red and blue voices. Never as a wash, glow, gradient background, or freestanding accent.
  • Keep red and blue abstract voices. Never render party iconography, flags, mascots, or campaign styling.
  • The isolated lens detail must never read as a leaf, petal, eye, badge, or sports mark.
  • Require non-music and non-formal-luxury companion imagery before treating the image system as proven.
  • No public use, delivery, or publication before explicit approval.

Avoid

  • question-mark silhouettes
  • professor cosplay
  • generic formal luxury
  • purple mysticism
  • moody tech-founder treatment
  • music-only storytelling
  • sterile AI or SaaS imagery
  • invented logo or pseudo-logo
  • political party iconography
  • labeled Venn diagrams and consulting charts
