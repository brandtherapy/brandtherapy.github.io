Using the OS

A reference for making every new asset feel unmistakably Bright Business.

01

Ask for the asset

Use this OS as the reference for your AI assistant. Describe what you need and provide the event facts. The strategy, identity and application rules govern the finished files.

  • Name the audience and the decision the asset should help them make.
  • Use the strategy and identity rules together.
  • Follow the relevant application example, adapting the content to the occasion.

02

Give the event facts

For an invitation, the necessary details are the program, topic, date, time and location. Add a speaker and sponsor when confirmed. The finished artwork should carry those facts clearly and accurately.

03

Keep the system consistent

  • Keep the company identity intact across every location.
  • Use the exact logo, typefaces and colour roles.
  • Choose a composition for the content, keeping the fixed canvas and safe margins.
  • Check the finished file at full size and at the size the audience will see.
