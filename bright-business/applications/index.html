Applications

Brand application across the four event programs.

01

Event design concepts

An illustrated world built around real business decisions, shared expertise and the next move. Each composition gives the Explorer a different role.

02

One brand across four programs

San Diego, Costa Mesa and Online share the Bright Business Club identity. The Marketing & Sales Group keeps its own program name within the same brand.

03

Campaign formats

A square starts the conversation. A story brings the idea closer. A cover identifies the event. A listing turns interest into attendance. Use the same visual idea across these formats, composing each for its setting.

04

From calendar to finished assets

Confirm the topic, date, time, venue and any speaker or sponsor details. The same identity rules govern the next invitation, the full monthly set and the August–July calendar.
