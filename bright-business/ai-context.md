# Bright Business

## Strategy

The place where owners and experts collaborate on the decisions that move the bottom line.

### Product

A decision-making and accountability system for business owners and experts

Bright Business combines a working room, the Bright Business Operating System, the Human-Centred Matrix and continuing accountability. The Club brings the people together. BBOS maps the business. The Matrix structures a decision. Bright Brain supports the work between meetings.

### People

Business owners building growth, value and exit readiness; experts building authority

Owners of established businesses, particularly those with $1M or more in revenue, need better decisions as they grow, transition or prepare to exit. Experts contribute one discipline in a room of owners and peers. Useful contribution builds authority, ambassadors and qualified warm introductions.

### Purpose

Improve the decision-making process

Bright Business exists to help members improve how they make decisions. Its founding belief is: “Businesses don’t fail. Decision-makers do.” That belief puts responsibility for judgment and action at the centre of the brand.

### Promise

The best decision, at the best time, for the best outcome.

Better decisions serve growth, business value and exit readiness for owners. Experts build authority through contribution, then earn ambassadors and qualified warm leads. The promise directs the quality of the work; it does not guarantee a financial outcome.

### Personality

Direct. Calm. Honest about certainty. On the journey.

Name the diagnosis and its cost. Explain why the recommendation fits. State what is known and what still needs testing. Speak as a capable navigator accompanying an owner who makes the decision.

#### Composition



- Start with one topic or decision. Make it the largest readable element.
- Use a clear grid, six percent minimum safe margins and consistent alignment.
- Group location and logistics; reserve a distinct place for partners and speakers.
- Keep the brand mark smaller than the topic. Place it on an uncluttered field.
- Cards may use a 25px radius; pills 40px; data marks remain square. Shadows belong only to cards.
- Artwork has a fixed canvas. Scale it as a whole; never reflow a finished banner.

#### Imagery



| Source | Treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Explorer illustration | Charcoal ink, loose pale sky and ground washes, one red-shirt protagonist. One understandable situation. |
| Real people and venues | Use verified, suitable source photography. Preserve the person and the place. |
| Product | Use an actual rendered screen. Never illustrate a fictional interface or member result. |
| Avoid | Stock boardrooms, glossy pseudo-3D, decorative gradients, fabricated people presented as speakers. |

#### Do / avoid




#### Accessible production



- Use charcoal at full strength for small information. Low-opacity charcoal is for rules and nonessential marks.
- Charcoal on paper exceeds 4.5:1. Red on paper is about 4:1, so do not use it for small body text.
- Give interface controls visible keyboard focus and descriptive labels.
- Write useful alt text for finished assets. Put event details in the accompanying text as well.
- Keep text live in source masters; supply fixed PDF and PNG outputs.

### Logo

One Bright Business shield across the ecosystem.

#### The existing mark

Use the supplied Bright Business artwork as one intact asset.


#### Placement



- Keep clear space of at least one shield-frame thickness, approximately 15.7% of shield width in the manual construction.
- Preserve the complete aspect ratio and all lettering. Do not redraw, retype, stretch or recolour.
- Use the available charcoal master on paper. On a charcoal composition, place the mark in a normal paper footer.
- Program and location names are typeset labels, not new logos.
- Use a licensed, supplied inverse master if one becomes available. Do not simulate one by filtering the charcoal asset.

#### Partners

Keep sponsor marks subordinate and separate from the Bright Business mark. Until an approved sponsor logo is supplied, typeset the sponsor name with its role. Kiln is the San Diego location sponsor; Serendipity Labs is the Costa Mesa location sponsor.


### Brand Signature

Red flag specifications and usage.

#### The BBVL flag

A red rectangle precedes the uppercase eyebrow. At the manual’s base size it is 30 × 8px, followed by an 11.5px bold label with 1.6px tracking. Scale the entire lockup together.


#### Red flag usage



- Use one flag to introduce the branded subject or series.
- Keep the rectangle solid red and square-edged. No texture, gradient, rounding or animation.
- Do not stretch it into a decorative stripe or repeat it beside every paragraph.
- In the geometric register, additional red identifies a real constraint. In the illustration register, the Explorer’s shirt may carry red.
- Maintain one identity across locations. A city never receives its own colour or mark.

#### The Human-Centred Matrix

This is Bright Business’s decision framework. Its orientation carries meaning.


#### The decision sequence



- Future State is north. Current State is south. How / PTP is west. What / ATP is east.
- WHO / UX stays in the blue centre. Do not rotate or mirror the matrix.
- Name the Weakest Link, then the Leverage Move. Show the formula last.
- The matrix and the Brand Therapy Focus Star have different jobs. Do not combine their geometry or labels.

#### Company description

Bright Business brings owners and experts together around decisions and accountability. Through the Club, the Bright Business Operating System, the Human-Centred Matrix and Bright Brain, members work on the decisions that support growth, business value and the next stage of their business.


#### Five examples



| Situation | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Diagnosis | Every decision still comes back to you. That slows the business down. Identify which decisions someone else can own. |
| Teaching | Start with the Current State. Define the Future State. Then decide what must change, how it will change and who owns the move. |
| Proof | A useful progress review names the original decision, the action taken and what happened. |
| Invitation | Bring a business decision to the room. Work it through with owners and experts. Visit a meeting. |
| Service | Thanks for the context. The first thing to clarify is who owns this decision. Once that is clear, we can work through the next move. |

#### Three corrections



| Avoid | Use | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Unlock your full potential. | Work through the decision holding your business back. | Replace an abstract promise with a concrete task. |
| Join an amazing network of game-changers. | Owners and experts collaborating on real business decisions. | Name who is there and what they do. |
| Guaranteed leads and 2X growth. | Build authority through useful contributions to the room. | Do not turn an intended outcome into a guarantee. |

#### Terms and rhythm



- Use Owner, Expert, Member and Club consistently. On owner pages, say owner rather than founder.
- Use Weakest Link, Leverage Move, Matrix Movement, Destination and Machine when the underlying framework is relevant. Explain an unfamiliar term on first use.
- Use Visit a meeting or Apply for a seat. Avoid generic join, sign up and register.
- Address the reader as you. Give the diagnosis in one sentence, name its cost, then explain the recommendation.
- Keep “You belong here” and “A society for business explorers” out of new company copy.
- Use Stay Bright in notes from Florin or the Club; it need not close every asset.

## Execution

- Read strategy and identity, then the requested application.
- Check required facts; ask only for missing information needed for the final asset.
- Choose the appropriate composition and render the finished files.
- Inspect output for hierarchy, typography, logo, signature, facts and clipping; fix issues and inspect again.

- Change strategy to suit a design.
- Invent dates, speakers, testimonials or results.
- Generate a replacement logo or a real-person likeness.
- Turn the deliverable into a client-operated template editor.
- Send or publish without exact final-content approval.
