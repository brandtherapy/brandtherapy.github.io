Red flag specifications and usage.

01

The BBVL flag

A red rectangle precedes the uppercase eyebrow. At the manual’s base size it is 30 × 8px, followed by an 11.5px bold label with 1.6px tracking. Scale the entire lockup together.

BRIGHT BUSINESS CLUB

02

Red flag usage

  • Use one flag to introduce the branded subject or series.
  • Keep the rectangle solid red and square-edged. No texture, gradient, rounding or animation.
  • Do not stretch it into a decorative stripe or repeat it beside every paragraph.
  • In the geometric register, additional red identifies a real constraint. In the illustration register, the Explorer’s shirt may carry red.
  • Maintain one identity across locations. A city never receives its own colour or mark.

04

The decision sequence

  • Future State is north. Current State is south. How / PTP is west. What / ATP is east.
  • WHO / UX stays in the blue centre. Do not rotate or mirror the matrix.
  • Name the Weakest Link, then the Leverage Move. Show the formula last.
  • The matrix and the Brand Therapy Focus Star have different jobs. Do not combine their geometry or labels.
