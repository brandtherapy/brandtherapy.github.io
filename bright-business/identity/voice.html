Identity

Voice and Writing

Direct. Calm. Honest about certainty. On the journey.

01

Four principles

PrincipleWriting behaviour
DirectName the diagnosis, the cost and the next action. Use plain verbs.
CalmKeep the sentence measured. Let the substance carry the urgency.
Honest about certaintyDistinguish what is known, estimated and still being tested. Explain why this recommendation.
On the journeySpeak alongside the owner. The owner makes the decision; the room improves it.

02

The core language

RoleLine
PositionThe place where owners and experts collaborate on the decisions that move the bottom line.
BeliefBusinesses don’t fail. Decision-makers do.
PromiseThe best decision, at the best time, for the best outcome.
JourneyBusiness is a journey.
SystemOne room. One system. One better decision, then the next.
DistinctionNot a networking group.
Sign-offStay Bright.

03

Company description

Bright Business brings owners and experts together around decisions and accountability. Through the Club, the Bright Business Operating System, the Human-Centred Matrix and Bright Brain, members work on the decisions that support growth, business value and the next stage of their business.

04

Five examples

SituationExample
DiagnosisEvery decision still comes back to you. That slows the business down. Identify which decisions someone else can own.
TeachingStart with the Current State. Define the Future State. Then decide what must change, how it will change and who owns the move.
ProofA useful progress review names the original decision, the action taken and what happened.
InvitationBring a business decision to the room. Work it through with owners and experts. Visit a meeting.
ServiceThanks for the context. The first thing to clarify is who owns this decision. Once that is clear, we can work through the next move.

05

Three corrections

AvoidUseReason
Unlock your full potential.Work through the decision holding your business back.Replace an abstract promise with a concrete task.
Join an amazing network of game-changers.Owners and experts collaborating on real business decisions.Name who is there and what they do.
Guaranteed leads and 2X growth.Build authority through useful contributions to the room.Do not turn an intended outcome into a guarantee.

06

Terms and rhythm

  • Use Owner, Expert, Member and Club consistently. On owner pages, say owner rather than founder.
  • Use Weakest Link, Leverage Move, Matrix Movement, Destination and Machine when the underlying framework is relevant. Explain an unfamiliar term on first use.
  • Use Visit a meeting or Apply for a seat. Avoid generic join, sign up and register.
  • Address the reader as you. Give the diagnosis in one sentence, name its cost, then explain the recommendation.
  • Keep “You belong here” and “A society for business explorers” out of new company copy.
  • Use Stay Bright in notes from Florin or the Club; it need not close every asset.
