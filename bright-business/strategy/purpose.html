Purpose

Improve the decision-making process

01

Improve the decision-making process

Bright Business exists to help members improve how they make decisions. Its founding belief is: “Businesses don’t fail. Decision-makers do.” That belief puts responsibility for judgment and action at the centre of the brand.

02

The compass

Every activity improves a decision, its timing or the accountability that follows.

03

Keep in

  • Decision-making as a practice that can improve.
  • An owner who remains responsible for the decision.

04

Keep out

  • Community, learning or networking replacing the central purpose.
  • Activities with no connection to the business decision.
