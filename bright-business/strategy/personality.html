Personality

Direct. Calm. Honest about certainty. On the journey.

01

Direct. Calm. Honest about certainty. On the journey.

Name the diagnosis and its cost. Explain why the recommendation fits. State what is known and what still needs testing. Speak as a capable navigator accompanying an owner who makes the decision.

02

The compass

The reader understands the issue, the reason and the next move.

03

Keep in

  • Plain language, specific diagnosis and reasons.
  • Human support alongside clear accountability.

04

Keep out

  • Hype, generic praise and theatrical certainty.
  • A guru who claims to know the owner’s business better than the owner.

05

The character behind the behaviour

Sage judgment leads: help the owner understand the decision and its consequences. Structure and action support that judgment; peer warmth keeps the conversation human. These are supporting lenses for the direct, calm voice.
