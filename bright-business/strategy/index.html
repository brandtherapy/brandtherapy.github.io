Strategy

Focus Star

Five connected decisions define what Bright Business stands for.

01

The position

The place where owners and experts collaborate on the decisions that move the bottom line.

02

One room. One system.

Part of the ecosystemIts job
Bright BusinessThe master brand. One connected ecosystem and one shield.
Bright Business ClubThe monthly working room with owners, experts and accountability.
BBOSThe business blueprint: dependencies, constraints and the business journey.
Bright BrainThe navigator for decision support and accountability between meetings.
2×2 Human-Centred MatrixThe decision method: current state, future state, how, what and who.
Growth-to-Exit SprintA focused owner engagement. The website currently presents a 90-Day Sprint; use the current offer name and terms when promoting a specific program.

03

Two audiences. Distinct value.

AudienceValueInvitation
OwnersAccelerate growth, increase business value and prepare for transition or exit through better, timely decisions.Visit a meeting
ExpertsBuild authority through contribution, earn ambassadors and qualified warm introductions.Apply for a seat
PartnersEarn credibility and relevant distribution by supporting qualified owners and useful work.Discuss a partnership

04

The working rhythm

  • Accountability: revisit the previous commitment.
  • Topic: examine one part of the business.
  • Challenge: work through a real decision. Close with a commitment.

05

The operating standard

Decisions and accountability are the owned ideas. Community, networking, learning and tools support them. They do not replace them.
