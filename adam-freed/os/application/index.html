LinkedIn headline

Independent cannabis data and operations advisor | Data Integrity

Short bio

I help founder-led cannabis companies get their numbers on speaking terms. When reports, systems, and handoffs disagree, I map the break and help teams build data they can trust.

LinkedIn About

Legal cannabis was supposed to make operations clearer. Many teams still run on half-matching reports, siloed spreadsheets, and dashboards that do not agree.

I am an independent cannabis data and operations advisor. I help founders and C-suite leaders of small-to-mid cannabis businesses replace guesswork with Data Integrity: one shared truth across systems, conversations, and evidence.

My authority comes from more than 20 years around the plant, including the legacy market before legalization, and more than 10 years in regulated retail, cultivation, and manufacturing. I will not dress up bad data or bend a finding to a preferred conclusion.

If your reports look fine but the underlying data does not agree, that is the work.

Speaker bio

Adam Freed is an independent cannabis data and operations advisor. He helps founder-led cannabis companies build Data Integrity across systems, conversations, and evidence, drawing on 20+ years around the plant and 10+ years in regulated operations.
