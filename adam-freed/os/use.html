AI

Use with AI

Give AI the right brand context, then review the result before using it.

On this page
  1. How it works
  2. Starter prompts
  3. Final check

01

How it works

  1. 01
    Open

    Open ChatGPT or Claude. Start a new chat. One chat per task. Long chats drift off voice.

  2. 02
    Paste

    Hit Copy the brain above and paste it as your first message. Send it before you ask for anything.

  3. 03
    Ask

    Say what you need in one line. Channel, topic, goal. Or grab a starter prompt below.

02

Starter prompts

Website and speaking

Advisory introduction

Brand Therapy OS: https://brandtherapy.florianp.com/adam-freed/os/
AI context: https://brandtherapy.florianp.com/adam-freed/os/ai-context.md

Write a short advisory introduction for me.

Context: [who I am meeting with  -  a cannabis founder, a conference organizer, a journalist]
What they need to know: [the one credential or experience most relevant to this context]

Follow the speaking and advisory application rules in this context. Write in first person. This is a draft. I will review before using it.

LinkedIn

LinkedIn finding post

Brand Therapy OS: https://brandtherapy.florianp.com/adam-freed/os/
AI context: https://brandtherapy.florianp.com/adam-freed/os/ai-context.md

Write a LinkedIn post based on this observation.

The observation: [what I noticed in a cannabis operation, a report, or an industry conversation]
The gap it reveals: [what this says about the state of cannabis data or operations]

Follow the LinkedIn channel rules in this context. Open with the finding. Short paragraphs. No questions as hooks. No em-dash. Draft only.

Speaking

Speaker bio

Brand Therapy OS: https://brandtherapy.florianp.com/adam-freed/os/
AI context: https://brandtherapy.florianp.com/adam-freed/os/ai-context.md

Write a [short / long] speaker bio for me.

Format needed: [50-75 words / 200-300 words / 100-word moderator intro]
Event context: [what kind of event, who the audience is]

Use only the credentials documented in this context. Do not inflate scope or claims. First-person for conversational contexts, third-person for formal bios. Draft only  -  I verify accuracy before submitting.

Writing and newsletter

Data insights article

Brand Therapy OS: https://brandtherapy.florianp.com/adam-freed/os/
AI context: https://brandtherapy.florianp.com/adam-freed/os/ai-context.md

Draft an article on this cannabis data or operations topic.

Topic: [the specific finding, gap, or pattern I want to write about]
Evidence I have: [what I have observed, what reports say, what I know from operations]
Audience: [founders and C-suite of small to mid-size cannabis businesses]

Follow the writing channel rules in this context. Declarative title. Open with the finding. Evidence before conclusion. One thesis. Flag any claims I need to source before this publishes. Draft only.

03

Final check

  • Use verified facts and current brand pages
  • Keep voice and visual rules consistent
  • Do not invent credentials, results, quotes, or endorsements
  • Never use the temporary stylescape as a face reference
