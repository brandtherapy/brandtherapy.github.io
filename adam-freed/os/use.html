Use with AI

Give AI the right brand context, then review the result before using it.

On this page
  1. How it works
  2. Ready-to-use prompts
  3. Final check

01

How it works

One focused task, the right context, one reviewable draft.

Use the attached Adam Freed Brand Therapy OS context as fixed guidance.
Open the context file

Paste the brand context into the chat before you ask for the draft. Every prompt below also starts with the live OS URL.

  1. 01
    Open

    Start a new chat for one task. Long chats drift off voice and rules.

  2. 02
    Paste

    Give the AI the brand context first, before you ask for anything.

  3. 03
    Ask

    Name the channel, format (Finding, Handoff, Operator Note, Website, Email, or Video), topic, and goal.

  4. 04
    Review

    Check facts, voice, images, and Connection Cable behavior before using anything.

02

Ready-to-use prompts

Copy a prompt, replace the brackets, and keep the result in draft until it is reviewed.

LinkedIn

LinkedIn Finding

Brand Therapy OS: https://brandtherapy.florianp.com/adam-freed/os/
AI context: https://brandtherapy.florianp.com/adam-freed/os/ai-context.md

Use the attached Adam Freed Brand Therapy OS context as fixed guidance.

Draft a LinkedIn Finding post.
Observation: [what I noticed in a cannabis operation, report, or industry conversation]
The gap it reveals: [what this says about data integrity or operations]
Desired reader action: [fix a source, align a handoff, or stop trusting a false dashboard]

Follow the LinkedIn Finding rules. Open with the finding. Short paragraphs. No engagement bait, invented stats, or consultant language. Draft only for human review.

LinkedIn

LinkedIn Handoff

Brand Therapy OS: https://brandtherapy.florianp.com/adam-freed/os/
AI context: https://brandtherapy.florianp.com/adam-freed/os/ai-context.md

Use the attached Adam Freed Brand Therapy OS context as fixed guidance.

Draft a LinkedIn Handoff post that moves one finding into a next step.
The finding already made: [one clear operational judgment]
What to hand off: [the decision, check, or action the reader should take next]
Audience: [operators, founders, or operators who own the numbers]

Keep Data Integrity voice. Separate evidence from assumption when useful. Do not invent outcomes. Draft only.

03

Final check

  • Never invent facts, credentials, clients, outcomes, quotes, or endorsements.
  • Never use the temporary stylescape as a face or crop reference.
  • Keep The Connection Cable as one physical link with one coral connector, never a logo.
  • AI drafts. A person approves. Publishing, sending, and client delivery remain separate decisions.
