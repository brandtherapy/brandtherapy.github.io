P5 / Personality

Sage-led authority with a friendly Ruler edge

Adam has a Sage primary archetype, with Ruler and Friend tied as balancing secondary archetypes. He is thoughtful, knowledgeable, honest, calm, authoritative, down-to-earth, witty, dependable, and trustworthy.

On this page
  1. What it means
  2. Do and avoid
  3. Put it to work

01

What it means

Ground every conversation in facts, context, and lived experience. Take the work seriously without taking himself too seriously.

02

Do and avoid

Do

  • Sage clarity: thoughtful, knowledgeable, honest, insightful, and grounded in facts.
  • Ruler standards: authoritative, confident, decisive, dependable, determined, and reliable.
  • Friend relatability: down-to-earth, loyal, trustworthy, and in the room with operators rather than above them.
  • Witty and fun as human texture, without making Jester the brand archetype.
  • First-person singular, short declarative sentences, and evidence that makes the point specific.

Avoid

  • Preacher, whistleblower, or activist framing.
  • Consultant-bro positioning: no methodology trademarking, no proprietary framework naming.
  • Cannabis lifestyle voice.
  • Insider cynicism without constructive alternative.
  • Cheerleader or advocate framing for the industry as a whole.

03

Put it to work

  • Strategy: start from the brand personality: Sage clarity, Ruler standards, and Friend relatability. Use the sliders only as tonal calibration.
  • Copy: use direct, specific sentences rooted in facts, context, and lived experience. Let wit stay light and earned.
  • Visuals: preserve calm, literal, rational, transparent, and talkative cues through the approved material-led identity and candid operator imagery.
  • Sales: show Adam as the experienced advisor who can name the problem plainly and set a credible standard for the work.
  • Content: teach from lived experience, make the reasoning visible, and avoid manufactured outrage or moral posturing.
