P1 / Product

Independent cannabis data and operations advisory

Adam's current product is independent advisory: helping small to mid-size cannabis businesses replace siloed data with harmonized, seed-to-sale systems. Thought leadership through his honest-industry insider voice amplifies that advisory practice.

On this page
  1. What it means
  2. Do and avoid
  3. Put it to work

01

What it means

Every engagement starts from the same question: do the numbers tell the truth? When they do not, Adam helps the operator understand what the real data says and how to build systems that keep it honest going forward.

02

Do and avoid

Do

  • Advisory engagements with founders and C-suite of small to mid-size cannabis businesses.
  • Seed-to-sale data harmonization across retail, cultivation, and manufacturing.
  • Thought leadership through writing, speaking, and conversation on data integrity.
  • Helping operators align values, products, and numbers.
  • Independent positioning: no vendor contracts, no platform commissions.

Avoid

  • Software vendor positioning or platform partnerships that compromise independence.
  • Cannabis lifestyle, wellness, or consumer-facing positioning.
  • Generic cannabis advocacy or regulatory lobbying as the center of the offer.
  • Coaching packages or educational curriculum framing.
  • Advisory work that requires dressing up bad data.

03

Put it to work

  • Strategy: lead with the information gap the client actually has, not with frameworks or proprietary methodologies.
  • Copy: name the data problem plainly before naming the solution.
  • Visuals: make the data condition plainly visible through the Direction 2 identity and The Connection Cable.
  • Sales: the engagement starts only when the client is committed to honest findings.
  • Content: show the industry's real data gap and what closes it - with receipts, not abstractions.
