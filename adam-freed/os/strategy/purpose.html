P3 / Purpose

To build a cannabis industry that earns trust through data integrity

Adam's purpose is a cannabis industry that earns broad trust from regulators, investors, and consumers by treating data as a shared source of truth, not a marketing tool. He believes the most important move the industry can make right now is fixing the information gap from the inside.

On this page
  1. What it means
  2. Do and avoid
  3. Put it to work

01

What it means

The legitimacy crisis in cannabis is, at its root, a data problem. Consumers hear one story from marketing, another from budtenders, a third from lab results. Regulators see reports that almost match. The industry will not earn durable trust until the numbers are honest from seed to sale.

02

Do and avoid

Do

  • The information gap between operators, regulators, investors, and consumers.
  • The legitimacy crisis in cannabis data: conflicting reports, mismatched dashboards, and opaque supply chains.
  • Long-term industry resilience over short-term optics.
  • Treating data integrity as a values decision, not just a compliance decision.
  • The honest operator as the example that pulls the ecosystem up.

Avoid

  • Medical claims of any kind.
  • Cannabis lifestyle or consumer-experience advocacy.
  • Regulatory lobbying or political advocacy as the center of purpose.
  • Anti-industry positioning: the purpose is industry improvement, not industry criticism.
  • Any purpose framing that turns Adam into an activist or whistleblower.

03

Put it to work

  • Strategy: keep the purpose visible in all writing and speaking as the reason the standard matters.
  • Copy: name the information gap plainly and connect it to industry legitimacy, not just individual business risk.
  • Visuals: show evidence and real industry context without advocacy-poster energy, using the approved material-led identity.
  • Sales: the purpose pre-qualifies clients who share the belief.
  • Content: write about the gap, why it persists, and what honest operators are already doing to close it.
