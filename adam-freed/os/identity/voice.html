Section B

Voice and Writing

Write with calm authority and keep the finding exact.

On this page
  1. Voice rules
  2. On-voice lines
  3. Avoid

01

Voice rules

State the problem plainly

Name the data issue before naming the solution.

Ground claims in facts

Use lived operating experience and clear reasoning.

Stay calm and exact

Speak as an independent insider, not a consultant bro.

Keep wit dry

Earn lightness. Do not force it.

02

On-voice lines

  • Your numbers should be on speaking terms.
  • The reports look fine. The underlying data does not agree.
  • Get the systems talking before you trust the dashboard.

03

Avoid

  • Cannabis lifestyle, wellness, or activist language
  • Inflated credentials or invented outcomes
  • Unlock, journey, elevate, transform, at the end of the day
