Your Strategy

The strategy behind Data Integrity

Positioning statement

Independent cannabis data and operations advisory for founders and C-suite leaders who need their numbers to agree with each other.

Adam works across retail, cultivation, and manufacturing, wherever seed-to-sale data has stopped telling one consistent story. He takes no vendor contracts and no platform commissions, so a finding stays his own.

The Focus Star

The whole brand in five lines

  1. Product Independent cannabis data and operations advisory.

    The product is not a software platform or a training course. It is Adam's independent judgment, applied to seed-to-sale data that no longer agrees with itself, and his willingness to report what he actually finds.

  2. People Founders and C-suite of small to mid-size cannabis businesses.

    Persona: The operator whose numbers stopped agreeing. Running a multi-site or multi-license operation where the data tools have not kept pace with the business, reports disagree, and audits feel like a threat.

  3. Purpose To build a cannabis industry that earns trust through data integrity.

    Legal cannabis promised operators clarity, safety, and accountability. Many are still trapped in half matching reports and dashboards that do not agree. Adam closes that information gap by treating data as a shared source of truth that every team reads the same way.

  4. Promise Data Integrity.

    Brand DNA: Data Integrity. Adam brings data alignment, visibility, and reliability to the point where operators can trust the numbers guiding the business. He will not dress up bad data or bend a finding to a preferred conclusion.

  5. Personality Sage-led authority with a friendly Ruler edge.

    Adam leads with a Sage's calm command of the facts, backed by a Ruler's insistence on doing it right and a Friend's willingness to sit across the table. Dry wit shows up as texture, never as the headline.

Brand archetype

The sage who holds the standard

Ten words define the brand: thoughtful, knowledgeable, honest, calm, authoritative, down-to-earth, witty, dependable, insightful, and trustworthy. The roles below follow from them.

  1. Sage. Leads. Calm authority built on more than 20 years around the plant and more than 10 years in regulated operations.
  2. Ruler and Friend. Balance the Sage. The Ruler insists on doing it right, the Friend keeps the conversation direct and human.
  3. Wit. Stays a trace. Dry humor shows up in the moment. It never becomes the headline.

What Adam is not: a preacher, a whistleblower, or a hype man. No activist framing, no manufactured outrage, no promise dressed up as certainty.

Voice principles

How it sounds

01

State the problem before the reasoning

Every piece names what disagreed before it explains why or what to do about it. The finding comes first, never buried under the fix.

The reports look fine. The underlying data does not agree.
02

Ground every claim in lived experience

Every claim traces back to something Adam has personally measured, fixed, or watched go wrong inside regulated operations, not an industry talking point he has not tested himself.

I have spent more than 20 years around the plant and more than 10 years inside regulated retail, cultivation, and manufacturing.
03

Speak like a calm, exact insider

The voice is independent and human. Calm and exact carry more weight than urgency ever could.

Get the systems talking before you trust the dashboard.
04

Take the work seriously, not himself

Data integrity is serious. Adam holds the standard without holding himself above it. Dry wit shows up as texture in the moment, never as the headline, and never at the expense of the finding.

If that is not what you are looking for, I am not the right advisor for this engagement.
