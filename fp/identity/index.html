Signature · Preview

Signature Preview.

Variants of the the viewfinder bracket. Each has a specific use case. Never decorative. Full construction spec: references/viewfinder.md.

Heading · 1-corner
Clarity
comes into focus.
Top-left only. Asymmetric L shape (border-top + border-left, 28x10px). Entry point marker for headings.
Focal · 2-corner
Top-right and bottom-left anchored to the focal rectangle inside an image. The same image stays visible outside the frame, blurred and darkened.
Image · 2-corner
Top-right and bottom-left sitting completely outside the image edge. No blur, no focal crop; used when the image itself should remain intact.
