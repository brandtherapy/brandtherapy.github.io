Use this OS with AI

The whole practice runs on one file.

Everything in this OS compiles into one file, ai-context.md. I paste it into ChatGPT or Claude and the AI writes on strategy, in my voice, under my channel rules. No file, no standard.

Open the file
AI-CONTEXT.MD10,405 WORDS~19K TOKENSONE PASTEEVERY CHANNEL

The loop.

Ten minutes from sitting down to a draft I can actually look at. Same loop every time.

01

Open

Open ChatGPT or Claude. Start a fresh chat. One chat per task. Long chats drift off voice and quietly forget the rules.

02

Paste

Hit Copy the brain above and send it as the first message, before I ask for anything. That one paste carries the strategy, the voice, and every channel rule.

03

Ask

Say what I need in one line. Channel, topic, goal. Or grab a starter prompt below and fill in the brackets.

Starter prompts.

Copy one, replace the brackets, send. Each prompt names its channel so the right rules load.

Social

LinkedIn observation

Write a LinkedIn post.

What I noticed this week: [one specific thing about a founder, a brand, or the market. The smaller and more concrete, the better.]
The reframe: [the brand point it leads to]

Follow the social channel rules in this context. Hook under 100 characters, one idea, end on a reframe and not a lesson. First person, dry, no hustle-culture lines. This is a draft for my review, not a final post.
Email

Brand Therapy Weekly

Draft this week's Brand Therapy Weekly email.

This week's idea: [one line]
The proof or example: [a real situation, mine or a client's, that earns the idea]

Follow the email channel rules in this context. Lowercase short subject line, open on the scene or the dry diagnosis, one idea per send, one clear next step for the reader. Draft only. I approve before anything sends.
Sales Deck

Discovery follow-up

Write a follow-up note after a discovery call.

Who I spoke with: [name, what they do]
What they actually said the problem is: [their words, not mine]
The next decision in front of them: [one thing]

Follow the sales-deck channel rules in this context. Restate their situation in their language before I recommend anything, keep it short, name one next step. Draft only, and never name pricing or a claim the source does not support without flagging it for me.

The standard still applies.

  • The AI drafts. I approve. Nothing publishes or sends straight from a chat.
  • Names, numbers, dates, pricing, and client details get checked against reality before anything goes out.
  • If it reads like a generic brand consultant, the file was not pasted. Back to step one.
  • Brand Therapy is a method, not a company. First person, "I", never "we", and never lead with "personal brand".
  • When the source changes, recopy the file. Old paste, old brand.
