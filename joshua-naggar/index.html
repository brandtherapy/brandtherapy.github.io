The Focus Star

The whole brand in five lines

  1. Product Joshua sells strategic counsel for cannabis businesses navigating high-stakes complexity, not a menu of disconnected legal tasks.

    The product is the advantage of having Joshua inside the decision: legal risk, operational reality, politics, relationships, and business judgment held together.

  2. People Joshua is for cannabis businesses and operators in it for the long run, not quick win opportunists or under resourced information gatherers.

    Persona: The Long-Run Operator. Running or owning a cannabis business with real licenses, real assets, and real regulatory exposure, not a side experiment.

  3. Purpose Joshua exists to help serious cannabis businesses navigate chaos without losing their advantage, ethics, or opportunity.

    Cannabis chaos is not a backdrop. It is the condition Joshua is built to navigate, guiding clients through uncertainty, regulation, risk, and hidden liabilities.

    Main Brand Archetype: Ruler. Mature authority with showman energy under control: command, competence, and charisma that make the counsel memorable.

Brand archetype

The mix behind the character

The character mixes Ruler, Hero, and Magician, weighted toward mature Ruler authority.

  1. Ruler leads. Standards, order, and a plan that holds, even under regulatory pressure.
  2. Hero and Magician carry it. Courage under real stakes, paired with seeing the hidden risk before anyone else does.
  3. Sage and Caregiver support. Strategic imagination and loyalty to the client's real interest, always in service of the counsel, never in front of it.
  4. Jester stays a trace. Enough humor to make the counsel memorable, never enough to undercut the command.

What Joshua is not: a rebel, a performer, or a comforter. The character leads with command and foresight, not defiance or reassurance.
